# App Advertisement — Creative Brief

**Concept title:** "The Room Changes"
**Length:** 30 seconds · **Format:** vertical 9:16 (Reels / TikTok / LinkedIn)
**Style:** Archetypal stock footage + on-screen text + cinematic music
**Tone:** Full goosebumps. Apple "Here's to the Crazy Ones" gravity. Inspiring, not comedic.

---

## The big idea

These traits — communication, storytelling, charm — aren't gifts a few people were
born with. They're skills the greats built. So can you.

- **Aspirational:** be like the greats.
- **Democratizing:** it's learnable — which is exactly what the app sells.

Structure: **start at greatness → pull down to "this is you."**

### Pillars (archetypes, never real celebrities — likeness/copyright risk)
1. **The Communicator** — communication (Obama-type energy)
2. **The Storyteller** — storytelling (Jobs-type energy)
3. **The Charmer** — charm / man↔woman connection (DiCaprio-type energy)

Humor lives as *texture* (a room/table laughing), not a pillar. No comedian on the pedestal.
Charisma is the umbrella; "greatness" is the word we reach for.

## THE SCRIPT (v2 — locked)

| Time | Visual (archetypal stock) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:03** | Black. Hold. Faint room tone. | *"There are people who change a room just by walking in."* |
| **0:03–0:07** | **Communicator** — figure mid-speech, side-lit; cut to captivated faces, still. | *"They speak."* → *"And the world leans in."* |

### Notes
- The pivot line splits across two beats: **"Some call it charisma"** rides the accelerating
  montage; **"History calls it greatness"** lands on the silence before the drop — that pause is
  where the goosebump lives.
- **Charmer beat = connection through wit** (she's into what he *said*), not conquest. Warm light
  (coffee shop / bar at golden hour), never a club/seduction vibe. This is the only explicit
  man↔woman moment and it carries the dating promise.
- The final line is the payload — it sells without a feature list.

## MUSIC

Build this FIRST. It's a music-driven edit — cut the video TO the music, not the reverse.
Required shape: **slow build → swell (~0:20) → brief near-silence → triumphant drop → clean resolve.**

### Where to get it (must be licensed for COMMERCIAL / ad use)
- **AI-generated:** Suno (paid), Soundraw (built for video, drag the climax point), Mubert, AIVA, Lyria.
- **Curated libraries:** Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed (~$10–20/mo).
- **Free:** YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music.
- **A person:** Fiverr "music supervisor" (~$10–50) or custom composer (~$50–150).

⚠️ Free tiers usually do NOT grant commercial use. Confirm the plan allows advertising use and
screenshot the license terms before putting ad budget behind the video.

### Prompt for AI music generator (paste into the description field)
> Cinematic inspirational instrumental. Slow emotional build with soft piano and warm strings,
> gradually rising tension and layering. A powerful orchestral swell around two-thirds in, followed
> by a brief moment of near-silence, then a triumphant, goosebump-inducing drop with full strings and
> a deep drum hit. Resolves clean and uplifting at the end. Epic, motivational, hopeful. No vocals,
> fully instrumental.

- Custom lyrics field: leave EMPTY (instrumental).
- Duration: 30s if settable. Genre: Cinematic/Orchestral/Epic. Mood: Inspiring/Uplifting/Epic.
- Generate 3–4 times; pick the one where the pause-then-drop gives chills. Swell should hit ~0:20.

## PRODUCTION WORKFLOW (in order)

1. **Lock the music first.** Pick/generate the track before touching footage.
2. **Map the beats.** Drop track in editor, mark build/swell/silence/drop. Script timings bend to
   the actual music.
3. **Gather footage to fill the beats.** Stock sources below. Pull 2–3 options per beat;
   over-collect on the man↔woman "genuine laugh" clip (hardest to find good).
4. **Rough cut to the beats.** Cut ON the music markers. No text yet — get visual rhythm right.
5. **Add text overlays.** Time lines to land before each cut and breathe. "History calls it
   greatness" sits on the silence/swell.
6. **Polish.** Warm cinematic color grade, subtle speed ramps, clean logo animation, sound-design
   whoosh into the drop.

### Footage sources (commercial-licensed)
- **Free:** Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit
- **Paid (more cinematic):** Storyblocks, Artgrid

### Editing tools
- **CapCut** (recommended first build — free, beat-detection, vertical + text + music in one place)
- **DaVinci Resolve** (free, pro color grading) · **Premiere Pro** (paid, industry standard)
- AI editors (Runway etc.) can auto-assemble/polish AFTER music + rough structure are locked.

## LEGAL GUARDRAILS (decided over the planning convo)

- **No real celebrity faces/clips** (Obama, DiCaprio, etc.) — right-of-publicity + copyright wall,
  especially for commercial/paid use. Invoke the *archetype*, not the identity.
- **No comedian/movie clips** in a paid ad — rejected at ad review; copyright exposure.
- **Music + B-roll must be royalty-free / commercially cleared.** Verify on the plan, not just "free."
- **Instagram in-app music:** personal accounts get full catalog (organic, non-promotional only);
  business accounts get the royalty-free Meta Sound Collection. Paid ads strip popular songs — use
  cleared/royalty-free audio.
- **AI editing your own footage = fine** (you own it). AI-*generated* new footage: check the tool
  grants commercial rights; never generate real people's likenesses. Label photorealistic AI in
  paid ads (Meta/TikTok requirement).
- **Organic posting risk:** low for one-off LinkedIn/IG posts (complaint-driven, under-enforced),
  but commercial use removes "fan/fair-use" goodwill. Paid ads = strict pre-approval; copyrighted
  assets get rejected and can suspend the ad account.
