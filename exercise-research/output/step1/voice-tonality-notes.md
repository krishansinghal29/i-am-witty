## What the prior agent had covered (so I deliberately avoided)
The first 68 were strong on: actor warmup canon (StageMilk/Backstage), Linklater/Berry/Rodenburg foundations (semi-supine, touch of sound, resonating ladder, one-thought-one-breath, walk-the-punctuation), broadcast/voiceover reads (Wikibooks Announcing, NPR, radio presenter), SLP staples (straw phonation, VFEs/Stemple, MPT, resonant voice chant), filler/fry/uptalk fixes, and Spolin gibberish/emotion games.

## Where the new 60 came from (richest sources, roughly ranked)
1. **Estill Voice Training (Wikipedia + estillvoice.com)** — figure-based isolation work was almost entirely absent before: silent-laugh retraction, twang (aryepiglottic narrowing), sob/larynx-lowering, onset trio, larynx-height slider, whisper-scream. Very productive, anatomically distinct drills.
2. **Speech-language pathology beyond the basics** — fluency-shaping & stuttering modification (easy onset, light articulatory contact, continuous phonation, pull-out/cancellation), resonance/hypernasality therapy (pinch-nose cul-de-sac, NG-to-AH), Froeschels chewing method, confidential voice, laryngeal/masseter massage, R-sound elicitation slides. theadultspeechtherapyworkbook.com, connectedspeechpathology.com, entokey.com were repeatedly useful.
3. **Accent modification / ESL prosody** (accentadvisor.com, speechstudio.ca, boldvoice.com) — minimal pairs, word linking/connected speech, stress-timing rubber band, intonation contour tracing. A whole productive vein the prior agent only touched via "shadowing."
4. **Trans voice training** (connectedspeechpathology.com MTF guide, reneeyoxon.com) — resonance brightening/darkening, whisper-scream larynx isolation, chest-resonance/optimal-pitch work. Surprisingly rigorous, pitch-vs-resonance-separated drills.
5. **Singing pedagogy adapted to speech** (ramseyvoice.com, bel canto/appoggio, messa di voce, Gilbert & Sullivan patter) — bratty-nay register connector, staccato-to-legato, messa di voce swell, appoggio rib suspension, patter-song speed drill. Plus chanting/overtone traditions (Om, Tuvan khoomei) for resonance awareness.

Also mined: Toastmasters Pathways vocal-variety project (4 Ps annotation, mood music read), Stanislavski tempo-rhythm, Fitzmaurice tremorwork, drama-classroom group games (Zip Zap Zop, the Machine, vocal conductor, sound ball), pause-craft articles (six minutes, speakupforsuccess), news-anchor/teleprompter routines, and physiology-based nerve resets (physiological sigh, calm-voice-under-nerves).

## Search queries that worked well
- Naming a *method/teacher* + "exercises" surfaced the richest distinct material: `Estill figures twang sob`, `Fitzmaurice tremor`, `Froeschels chewing voice therapy`, `Lessac`, `Stanislavski tempo-rhythm`.
- `<problem> exercises` framings: `reduce nasal voice exercises pinch nose`, `stop talking too fast chunking pause`, `fluency shaping easy onset light contact`.
- Sub-niche population names: `trans voice training pitch resonance drills`, `accent reduction minimal pairs linking`, `overtone throat singing`.
- Mining a found exercise name as the next query (e.g. "appoggio" → bel canto support; "bratty nay" → register-connection singing drills).

## Sources found but not fully mined (future depth)
- **Estill Level 1/2 workbooks** (PDFs on Scribd) — the full 13 figures each have multiple sub-options/recipes I summarized rather than exploded into separate drills; could yield 5-10 more if split finely.
- **Linklater's full progression** (archive.org full text) and **Rodenburg's books** — I captured the headline drills (channel of sound, tongue stretch) but the day-by-day workbook sequences contain many micro-exercises.
- **Cicely Berry, *Text in Action* / *The Working Shakespeare* video series** — physical-text drills (pushing against a wall on a line, passing a ball on stressed words, whispering across a room) only partly captured.
- **Roy Hart / extended-voice workshop syllabi** — vocal-archetype and full-range "find sounds you didn't know you had" exploration is hard to render as a stepwise solo drill; left mostly uncaptured.
- **filetype:pdf drama/speech teacher handouts** — several (Oxford, StageMilk drama school, Fort Bend ISD, ArtFormsLeeds "lips teeth tip of tongue") returned but were binary/garbled on fetch; their twister lists and warmup sequences likely hold a few more.
- **Voiceover practice-script PDFs** (School of Voiceover 84 scripts, etc.) — these are *material* to read aloud rather than new drill *types*, so deliberately skipped per the "exercises not products" rule.

## Honest coverage gaps / under-covered sub-areas
- **Singing-technique-for-speakers** could go deeper (vibrato control, register "mix," head-voice access for speech, lip-trill scales on specific patterns) — I added the highest-value ones but a vocal-coach could extract more.
- **Extended/experimental voice** (Roy Hart, Grotowski resonators, Linklater's "freeing the wild voice") is thin — these are powerful but resist clean stepwise instructions and benefit from a teacher; only lightly represented.
- **Group/ensemble vocal games** got a solid boost (Zip Zap Zop, Machine, Conductor, Sound Ball, Pass the Sound) but improv-archive and drama-notebook have more (e.g. "Vowel Movement," "1-2-1," polyphonic round-singing) that could be added.
- **Measurement/biofeedback tools** (s/z ratio, nasalance, pitch-app targets, formant visualization) are present as a couple of drills but could be a small dedicated cluster.
- **Hecklers / curveball delivery under pressure** is more a quick-wit/oratory concern; voice-specific "stay-supported-when-rattled" work is represented by the nerve drills but is thin.
- Non-English-origin traditions beyond chanting/overtone (e.g. Kalari/Indian voice culture, Noh/Kyogen vocal production, African call-and-response work-song forms) are essentially untouched and could add genuinely fresh, non-Western drills.
