## Richest sources (5)
1. **CBT-for-social-anxiety self-help modules & clinics** — CCI "Stepping Out of Social Anxiety", National Social Anxiety Center, Social Anxiety Alliance UK, Oxford CADAT self-study guide. Deep, structured, evidence-based behavioral experiments.
2. **UCLA PEERS curriculum (Laugeson)** — concrete, rule-and-step social drills (trading information, entering/exiting conversations) that translate cleanly into runnable exercises.
3. **Improv-for-dating writeups + improv encyclopedias** (emlovz, Grotto, ImprovEncyclopedia, improwiki) — named games with clear procedures and a dating angle.
4. **Authentic Relating / Circling materials and psychodrama/Gestalt literature** (PositivePsychology, GoodTherapy) — relational and rehearsal exercises with strong vulnerability/presence training.
5. **Social-skills challenge communities & coaches' free articles** (Nick Notas, Craft of Charisma, Good Looking Loser, happyshypeople, succeedsocially, aboutsocialanxiety comfort-zone lists) — high volume of concrete field drills and graduated ladders.

## Search queries that worked well
- Naming a specific framework + "exercises/drills/practice" (e.g., "PEERS trading information two-way conversation handout"; "NVC observation feelings needs requests practice pairs").
- Mining an exercise name as its own query (Failure Bow, Story Spine, In Medias Res, physiological sigh, people scavenger hunt).
- "comfort zone challenge list 30 day" and "approach anxiety baby steps" surfaced granular dares/ladders.
- Pairing a clinical model term with "behavioral experiment" surfaced the deliberate-mistake / drop-safety-behavior drills.

## Honest coverage gaps / under-covered sub-areas
- **Queer / non-heteronormative dating dynamics** and **older-adult / re-entry dating** have almost no dedicated exercises here; most field drills assume a young, hetero, bar/daytime-approach frame.
- **Sexual communication & consent rehearsal** is touched only via the touch ladders; explicit consent-conversation and desire-stating drills are thin.
- **Long-term relationship / post-date relating** is mostly out of scope (correctly), but the boundary with "conversation" domain is fuzzy — several assertiveness/listening drills could live in either.
- **Reading others' interest signals** (decoding IOIs, disinterest, polite-no cues) is practiced incidentally inside other drills but lacks a dedicated observation-and-label exercise.
- **Texting/online-dating** is decently covered but could go deeper on photo selection, voice/video-first apps, and de-escalation after ghosting.
- The two **binary PDFs** (PEERS, teach-this small talk) remain the most likely place to find 3-5 more genuinely new rule-based drills if read directly.
