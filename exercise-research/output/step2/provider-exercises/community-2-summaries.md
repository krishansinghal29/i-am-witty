# Step 2b Provider Summaries — community-2

## Ditch the Label — 28 Days of Rejection Therapy
- **Methodology:** Behavioral exposure / rejection-desensitization ladder for a youth audience. One escalating social challenge per day for 28 days, moving from tiny low-stakes asks (directions, compliments) to higher-stakes social/romantic risks (ask your crush out, share creative work), then a reflection-and-consolidation week, plus an ongoing "rejection journal" bonus.
- **Exercises found:** 24 (Days 1–21 individually, Days 22–27 as one reflection block, Day 28, plus the bonus journal). The full day-by-day list was recovered.
- **Evidence quality:** Mostly confirmed — every challenge was extracted verbatim from the live page's embedded list (the page is JS-rendered; pulled from the Wayback-archived HTML source). Source: https://ditchthelabel.org/28-days-of-rejection-therapy-challenges
- **Format relevance to riffy:** Very high — this is exactly a ready-made challenge ladder for the dating-social/quick-wit domain, free, and directly translatable into field-assignment cards.
- **2c deep scrape:** No. The whole list is already captured here; a deeper scrape would add nothing. The content is the 24 items above plus the journal/reflection framing.

## Hot Breath! Comedy Network
- **Methodology:** Two-pronged — (1) the Hot Breath! podcast (host Joel Byars, 400+ interviews) mining working comedians' techniques, and (2) Hot Breath! Pro ($149 lifetime), which bundles the Comedy Writing Masterclass taught by Jerry Corley ("The Joke Doctor") plus a "Comedy Writing Room" community for peer joke punch-up. Theory of improvement: learn joke mechanics (setup-punchline-tag; assumption then surprise; premise = topic + condition; compare/contrast and ~13 named comedy structures) and drill volume via daily/timed joke writing.
- **Exercises found:** 7 (setup-punchline-tag, assumption/reinterpretation, premise building, speed/daily writing, compare-and-contrast structure, the community writing room, podcast technique-mining).
- **Evidence quality:** Mixed-to-confirmed. The joke-structure exercises are confirmed via Corley's Stand Up Comedy Clinic materials (which power the masterclass) and the masterclass page. The Pro membership price and Comedy Writing Room are confirmed/lightly-inferred (the Podia store and pro pages partly blocked fetching).
- **2c deep scrape:** Optional/medium. The Stand Up Comedy Clinic free-article library (standupcomedyclinic.com) and the "13 Comedy Structures" PDF are the richest, most drillable source — start there if a comedy-exercise deep dive is wanted: http://weekend-workshop.s3.amazonaws.com/13%20COMEDY%20STRUCTURES.pdf and https://www.standupcomedyclinic.com/. The Hot Breath site itself is largely a podcast + paywalled course, so less to scrape.

## Story District (Washington DC)
- **Methodology:** Coached, cohort-based personal-storytelling progression (true-story stage performance, Moth-style). Three structured stages: Foundations (4wk, no performance) → Storytelling 101 (7wk, ends in showcase) → Performance Lab/201 (6wk, one story to performance). Improvement comes from narrative-arc instruction, scene-building (tension/stakes), 1:1 coaching, recorded-feedback iteration, and a live capstone show.
- **Exercises found:** 6 (the three courses plus the cross-cutting practices: recording-feedback, 1:1 coaching, final showcase).
- **Evidence quality:** Confirmed at the course/structure level (durations, weekly cadence, what students produce, coaching/recording/showcase components all on official pages). Week-by-week micro-exercise breakdown is not public, so individual in-class drills are inferred.
- **Format relevance to riffy:** High for the storytelling domain — the arc → scene → stakes → deliver-and-record loop is a clean template, though it's live/coached rather than self-serve.
- **2c deep scrape:** Low/no. The public pages give the structure; the actual weekly drill sheets are gated inside the paid classes and aren't scrapable. Start URL if pursued: https://www.storydistrict.org/master-the-art-of-storytelling

## Business of Story (Park Howell)
- **Methodology:** A single, highly drillable atomic exercise — the ABT (And, But, Therefore) framework (from Dr. Randy Olson) — taught as the universal three-act spine for any message. Improvement = write ABTs over and over (agreement → contradiction → consequence) until automatic, then graduate to the 10-step Story Cycle System for longer brand stories. Podcast (free) + The Narrative Gym for Business (75pp) + ABTs of Selling mini-course (18 min) carry the method.
- **Exercises found:** 5 (write-an-ABT, ABTs of Selling mini-course, Narrative Gym worksheets, Story Cycle 10-step, famous-speech ABT analysis).
- **Evidence quality:** Confirmed for the ABT framework and the named products; Story Cycle and the analysis drill are inferred from descriptions/podcast format.
- **Format relevance to riffy:** Very high — the ABT is the single most "atomic, repeatable" exercise in this whole batch and maps perfectly to a quick writing-prompt drill (fill in And/But/Therefore).
- **2c deep scrape:** Yes (lightweight). The blog/podcast are full of worked ABT examples ideal for generating prompt+example pairs. Start URL: https://businessofstory.com/powerful-business-storytelling-starts-with-the-abt/ (and the broader businessofstory.com blog).

## PSDA — Professional Speech & Debate Association
- **Methodology:** Adults-only weekly speech-and-debate practice community ($95/mo). Reps-through-competition model: weekly live IPDA-style debates + recurring speech/debate events, a monthly 2-hour intensive, and expert coaching. Strong on impromptu/quick-wit via Spontaneous Speech (7-min prompt-to-stage), SPAR, Impromptu (noun/fact/quote draws), Spontaneous Debate, and extemp, balanced by Prepared Speech and Prepared Acting.
- **Exercises found:** 9 (Spontaneous Speech, Impromptu, SPAR, Spontaneous Debate, IPDA debate, Prepared Speech, Extemp, Prepared Acting, monthly intensive).
- **Evidence quality:** Mixed-to-confirmed. The spontaneous formats, IPDA, prompts, 7-min rule, and intensive are confirmed via the about page and tabroom tournament invites; Extemp and Prepared Acting are inferred from event listings.
- **Format relevance to riffy:** High for quick-wit/oratory — the prompt-to-impromptu-speech format (concrete noun / abstract noun / fun fact / quotation draw, short timer) is directly drillable and AI-roleplayable.
- **2c deep scrape:** Yes (lightweight). The tabroom tournament invites carry the precise event rules/formats and prompt categories worth mining. Start URL: https://www.prosda.com/events and the linked tabroom invites (e.g. tourn_id=26799).
