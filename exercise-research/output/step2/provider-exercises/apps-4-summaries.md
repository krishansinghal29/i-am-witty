# apps-4 — Provider Summaries

## TED Masterclass
- **Methodology**: Structured, single-deliverable curriculum. 11 animated lessons hosted by Chris Anderson, organized in 3 phases — Identify Your Ideas (L1–3), Plan Your Approach (L4–5), Develop Your Talk (L6–11). The whole course is a scaffold for building one TED-style talk end to end.
- **Theory of improvement**: Curriculum + guided creation, not reps or AI feedback. You learn a principle per lesson (idea selection, through-line, scripting, voice/presence, connection, storytelling, explanation, persuasion, revelation, visuals) and immediately apply it to your own talk.
- **Exercises found**: 12 (11 lessons + 1 build-your-own-talk capstone). All confirmed from the public course-overview page.
- **Hands-on work**: brainstorm an idea list, pick a through-line, write a talk plan, rehearse delivery, design visuals. No AI/recording feedback loop evident — feedback comes from the framework, not the app.
- **Evidence quality**: Mostly confirmed (official curriculum page lists every lesson by name and the assignment in each phase).
- **2c deep-scrape**: Worthwhile — it is a clean, fully-named structured program and a good source of an "idea → through-line → delivery" exercise sequence. Start URL: https://masterclass.ted.com/course-overview

## Ovation VR
- **Methodology**: AI-simulation reps. Desktop + VR speaking simulator where you rehearse against responsive AI avatars in realistic venues and get analytics-based coaching. No fixed curriculum — it's a practice environment, not a course.
- **Theory of improvement**: Exposure + pressure (graduated audience sizes/venues) plus objective delivery feedback. Avatars create social pressure and ask context-aware follow-up questions.
- **Exercises found**: 12 distinct modes/scenarios (Speech+Q&A, presentation rehearsal, conversation, job interview, difficult conversations/conflict, sales pitch, elevator pitch, debate, profession-specific roleplays, custom Persona design, venue/audience-pressure rehearsal, delivery-analytics review).
- **Customization is itself the design lever**: tune avatar mood/tone/role/pushback; ~14 venues; audience up to thousands. These knobs are directly reusable as exercise-difficulty parameters.
- **Feedback**: transcript, filler words, pacing, gaze distribution across audience, mic distance, customizable AI coaching notes.
- **Evidence quality**: Core scenarios + analytics confirmed from ovationvr.com and RoadToVR review; elevator-pitch/debate/therapy-medical-language roleplays inferred from secondary review sites.
- **2c deep-scrape**: Medium-yes — not a structured program, but the scenario/persona/venue parameter matrix is high-value for drill design. Start URL: https://www.ovationvr.com/individuals (and the Personas/blog page).

## LikeSo
- **Methodology**: Lightweight speech-fitness drilling. Two modes only, scored by on-device voice recognition. Designed as a quick "speak and get a grade" loop, not a curriculum.
- **Theory of improvement**: Reps + immediate quantified feedback on filler words and pace; repetition drives down filler usage and stabilizes pacing (~150 wpm target).
- **Exercises found**: 3 — TalkAbout (12 topic prompts × 5-prompt sessions, talk-on-the-fly game), FreeStyle (up-to-30-min open-mic with user-selected target words), and the Articulate Score feedback engine (A+ to "Uh Oh!").
- **Most reusable idea**: TalkAbout's topic-roulette format (Speed Dating, Job Interview, Debate Team, Small Talk, etc.) → directly maps to quick-wit/conversation drill prompts. Full 12-topic list is partially confirmed (5+ named, rest unconfirmed).
- **Evidence quality**: Confirmed from official how-it-works page and App Store listing.
- **2c deep-scrape**: No — small surface area, already fully understood. Only loose end is the complete 12-topic list, obtainable from the App Store screenshots if needed. Start URL (only if pursuing topic list): https://apps.apple.com/in/app/likeso/id1074943747

## Charisma Virtual Social Coaching (Center for BrainHealth, UT Dallas)
- **Methodology**: Clinician-guided, evidence-based virtual social coaching. 10 hours over 5–10 weeks, $1,495. Live coach + Unreal-Engine avatar role-play; "learning through doing." Targets ASD, social anxiety, ADHD, learning differences.
- **Theory of improvement**: Teach a social-cognitive strategy, then immediately practice it in role-play with interspersed live coaching. Research version teaches nine named strategies; consumer framing uses four pillars (Curiosity, Connection, Courage, Charisma). Progress measured by the I-ASSER2T instrument.
- **Exercises found**: 6 — avatar social role-play, strategy-training-with-interspersed-coaching, initiating-conversations drill, maintaining-relationships/interpersonal-dynamics, and population-specific tracks (Transition to Adulthood, Children & Families).
- **Evidence quality**: Methodology and skill targets confirmed (program site + peer-reviewed EurekAlert release). The specific nine strategies and the individual scenario scripts are NOT public — that detail lives in research papers.
- **2c deep-scrape**: Medium-yes, but pivot to the literature, not the marketing site. The clinical protocol (the nine strategies + scenario design) is the valuable artifact. Start URL: https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/973069 → trace to the cited journal article (search "Charisma virtual social coaching nine social cognitive strategies" on PubMed/Google Scholar).
