# Step 2b Provider Summaries — Batch: apps-1

Batch of 7 AI/app speaking & social-confidence providers. Per-exercise detail in `apps-1.jsonl`.

---

## Yoodli
- **Methodology:** Reps + AI feedback. No fixed curriculum; the unit of practice is a *recorded session* or an *AI roleplay*. You speak, the AI analyzes (filler words, pacing, conciseness, word choice, eye contact, body language) and, in roleplay, talks back in-character and adapts in real time.
- **Exercise types found (5):** Speech/Pitch Practice, Interview Practice (incl. multi-person panels, paste-a-JD), Conversation/Roleplay Practice, Sales Roleplay scenarios (inbound/outbound discovery, objection handling, multi-persona buying committee), and the Roleplay Builder for custom scenarios.
- **Evidence quality:** Mostly **confirmed** — official support docs, feature-announcement pages, and the AI-roleplays product page all describe modes directly.
- **Domains hit:** oratory, conversation, some dating-social (difficult conversations). Heavily professional/enterprise-skewed (sales, interviews, manager talks).
- **2c warranted?** Borderline-yes for *roleplay-scenario taxonomy* but the library is templated and enterprise-flavored, not a fixed program. Best single URL: `https://yoodli.ai/blog/sales-roleplays` + the premade roleplay library inside `app.yoodli.ai`. Strong reference for AI-feedback *metrics* design rather than exercise catalog.

## Vocal Image
- **Methodology:** Structured, plan-driven voice training. AI evaluates your voice, then generates a personalized daily plan (5-10 min) and multi-week themed challenge programs; "watch & repeat" drills you do at home. This is the most curriculum-like provider in the batch on the *voice* axis.
- **Exercise types found (7 buckets, ~30+ named drills):** AI Voice Evaluation, Daily Plan, Breathing/Diaphragm drills (Inner push, Hiss attack, Buzz charge, Breath control), Resonance/Depth drills (Opera flow, Cosmic hmm, Libra, Figure 8 sound, etc.), Articulation drills + Tongue Twisters (Articulate!, Tongue flow, Jaw massage, Wow workout), 8 themed Challenge programs, and a Voice Tests library.
- **Evidence quality:** Mostly **confirmed** — official course page (`/courses/8-vip/`) names individual drills; review sites corroborate the daily-session structure.
- **Domains hit:** voice-tonality primarily (plus oratory/conversation framing). Not wit/humor.
- **2c warranted?** **YES.** A structured program with named, sequenced drills. Start URL: `https://www.vocalimage.app/en/courses/8-vip/` (Ultimate Challenge) and the courses index `https://www.vocalimage.app/en/courses/`. Best in batch for a granular voice-drill taxonomy.

## Orai
- **Methodology:** Gamified, progressive micro-lessons + reps with AI feedback. Unlock lessons one technique at a time (storytelling, confidence, conciseness, etc.); 5 min/day; app adapts difficulty and recommends next lesson.
- **Exercise types found (5):** Gamified Micro-Lessons, Freestyle Practice (impromptu), Script Mode (rehearsal vs. transcript), Scenario Practice (storytelling/persuasive/interview), Custom Filler-Word Flagging drill.
- **Evidence quality:** Mixed — lessons/metrics **confirmed** via App Store + site; the exact lesson *list* and named scenarios are **inferred** (no public curriculum page enumerates every lesson).
- **Domains hit:** oratory primarily, some storytelling/quick-wit (impromptu).
- **2c warranted?** **Maybe** — the gamified-lesson progression is relevant but the full lesson list isn't exposed publicly; a 2c would need app-internal screenshots/walkthrough video mining. Start URL: `https://apps.apple.com/us/app/orai-improve-public-speaking/id1203178170` + YouTube walkthroughs. Lower priority than Vocal Image/Ultraspeaking.

## Speeko
- **Methodology:** Daily warm-ups + big exercise library (1,000+) + AI feedback, with an AI-roleplay layer (Convos) and live/1:1 coaching. Bite-sized (2 min) and habit-oriented (streaks).
- **Exercise types found (7):** Daily Vocal Warm-Ups, Speaking Practice w/ real-time feedback, Random Topic Generator (impromptu), Picture Description, List Exercise, Convos AI Roleplay (interviews, negotiations, networking, small talk, difficult conversations), Personalized Courses (100+ topics incl. storytelling/impromptu/nerves), plus Live Events / 1:1 coaching.
- **Evidence quality:** Mostly **confirmed** — App Store listing + review sites name specific exercise types (Random Topic, Picture, List, Convos).
- **Domains hit:** oratory, voice-tonality, conversation, quick-wit (impromptu/picture). Good breadth.
- **2c warranted?** **Maybe-yes** for the *exercise-type catalog* (Random Topic, Picture, List, Convos are distinctive prompt formats directly relevant to wit). Hard to enumerate all 1,000+ items; a 2c would mine the App Store screenshots + a walkthrough video. Start URL: `https://www.speeko.co/` + `https://apps.apple.com/us/app/speeko-ai-for-public-speaking/id1071468459`.

## Ultraspeaking
- **Methodology:** Speaking-as-games. Built by two World Championship of Public Speaking finalists (featured in *Ultralearning*). Core thesis: take the "thinking brain" out of speaking via fast, low-stakes games that create "turbulence" and force flow. Delivered as a free game app + paid membership (daily 30-min live Zoom game sessions) + cohort courses. Each game isolates one skill, organized under principles: Speak Before You Think, Choose One Thing, Breathe, Feel What You Say, End Strong.
- **Exercise types found (11+ named games):** Rapid Fire (Analogies/Autocomplete), Triple Step, Conductor, Snow Globe, The One Thing, Bow & Arrow, Ending Strong, Flow, Own It, Breaking Character, Pillars — plus the daily live game sessions / cohort courses.
- **Evidence quality:** Mixed-to-good. Rapid Fire, Triple Step, Conductor, Snow Globe are **confirmed** with mechanics (dedicated pages + podcast/clone descriptions). The One Thing, Bow & Arrow, Ending Strong, Flow, Own It, Breaking Character, Pillars are **inferred** (named in lists/principles; mechanics not individually documented publicly — they live behind the app login at `app.ultraspeaking.com`).
- **Domains hit:** quick-wit, improv, oratory, voice-tonality — the **single most wit-relevant provider in the batch.** Game mechanics (analogies under time pressure, distraction management, energy-on-command) map almost directly onto wit training.
- **2c warranted?** **YES — highest priority.** Two start points: (1) the free clone `https://speaking.games/` (already yields 11 game descriptions across Confidence/Clarity/Delivery — Impromptu, Slides from Hell, Impossible Questions, Explain to a Child, Story Spark, Analogy Machine, Framework Master, Compressor, Tongue Twister Battle, Character Swap, Emotion Switch); (2) `https://app.ultraspeaking.com/games/practice/daily` for the canonical game set. A 2c should fully document every game's mechanics + difficulty settings.

## VirtualSpeech
- **Methodology:** Structured courses (video lessons) wrapped around hands-on VR/AI practice. The exercise unit is a *simulation*: deliver to a VR audience, or converse with an AI avatar, then get AI feedback + an AI-coach ("Hugh") debrief. 55+ hands-on exercises, 50+ roleplay scenarios, 15+ languages, certificate on completion.
- **Exercise types found (5 buckets):** VR Audience Simulation, Interview Roleplays (7+ variants incl. resume+JD, panel/multi-avatar, STAR, real company Qs), Soft-Skills AI Roleplay Library (customer service, debating/negotiation, difficult conversations, sales, networking, leadership), Roleplay Studio (custom builder), and the structured courses themselves.
- **Evidence quality:** Mostly **confirmed** — interview-practice page enumerates exact scenario variants; AI-practice + homepage confirm categories. The full 50-scenario list isn't individually enumerated publicly (some **inferred** at the item level).
- **Domains hit:** oratory, conversation, dating-social (difficult conversations/networking). Professional-skewed.
- **2c warranted?** **Maybe** — the course catalog is public and structured, good for a roleplay-scenario taxonomy, but VR-centric and professional, less wit-relevant. Start URL: `https://virtualspeech.com/courses` (route was flaky; use `https://virtualspeech.com/ai-practice` + `https://virtualspeech.com/practice/job-interview`). Medium priority.

## Rejecto
- **Methodology:** Real-world exposure therapy, gamified. No in-app drills — the "exercise" is a real-life social challenge you go do, then mark complete. 100-200+ challenges on a difficulty ladder (easy -> medium -> extreme) for gradual desensitization; points/streaks/badges + a supportive community for accountability. Directly embodies a "lower the stakes through play" ethos via a challenge ladder.
- **Exercise types found (3):** Rejection Challenge Ladder (the catalog of real-world dares), Daily Challenge & Streak system, Supportive Community sharing.
- **Evidence quality:** **Confirmed** at the structural level (App Store + Google Play + review sites describe the ladder, difficulty tiers, gamification, community) with example challenges; the *full* challenge list is **inferred** (not published in full). Note: the App Store listing has been re-themed toward a women's relationship/"Alora" positioning in some regions, so positioning is in flux.
- **Domains hit:** dating-social, conversation. Most directly relevant to the "lower the stakes" / exposure ethos, less so to verbal wit per se.
- **2c warranted?** **YES (light).** Worth scraping the full challenge ladder by tier — it's a ready-made field-assignment exercise catalog and difficulty-design reference. Start URL: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.betterappsbetteryou.rejecto&hl=en_US` + App Store screenshots + the Rejecto YouTube channel (`https://www.youtube.com/@Rejectoapp`) which walks through specific challenges.
