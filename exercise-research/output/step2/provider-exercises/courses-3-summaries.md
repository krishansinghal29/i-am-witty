# Step 2b Provider Summaries — courses-3

## Duarte — Story Skills / Presentation Training
- **Methodology**: Structured, research-backed corporate curriculum (35+ yrs, Nancy Duarte's books Resonate/Slide:ology). Theory of improvement = audience empathy first, then narrative structure (contrast), then visual storyboarding. Heavy on frameworks and tooling rather than reps.
- **Signature exercises**: Move From → Move To audience map, Big Idea statement, Sparkline ("what is / what could be") mapping, sticky-note storyboarding, analog visual sketching, S.T.A.R. moment design, DataStory exec-summary structuring.
- **Exercise count found**: 7 distinct activities.
- **Evidence quality**: Mostly confirmed (Sparkline, sticky-noting, Move From/Move To, audience empathy all documented on duarte.com + books). Big Idea and S.T.A.R. inferred from the Resonate book/framework rather than seen in a public curriculum page.
- **Format/pricing**: Instructor-led workshops (in-person, virtual) + self-paced online; B2B-priced (~$500–1000+/seat). Worksheets are physical (sticky notes, marker pens).
- **2c deep scrape?**: MEDIUM-NO. The method is well-known and exercise mechanics are concrete, but the actual workshop worksheets are gated behind lead-gen/enterprise sales — a public scrape won't surface much more than the book already gives. Start URL if pursued: https://www.duarte.com/resources/guides-tools/resonate-workshop-overview/

## MasterClass — Communication cluster (Roberts, Voss, Burton, Stephanopoulos, McConaughey)
- **Methodology**: Inspiration-first celebrity video lessons (~10-min chapters, ~2–3 hrs/class). Light structured practice — each class ships a workbook with reflection prompts; some classes (Voss) include demonstrated mock scenarios + community-run live practice. Not a reps engine.
- **Signature exercises**: Voss mock salary negotiation roleplay; mirroring/labeling/calibrated-question drills; Burton relaxation + performative-delivery (rhythm/pacing/pause) exercises; Roberts authentic-communication workbook prompts; generic per-class workbook reflections.
- **Exercise count found**: 5 (cluster-level; varies per instructor).
- **Evidence quality**: Mixed — Voss and Burton exercises confirmed via chapter pages/reviews; Roberts and the general workbook prompts inferred (MasterClass workbooks exist but content not publicly enumerated).
- **Format/pricing**: Subscription (~$10–20/mo annual). Mostly watch-and-reflect; minimal interactivity beyond optional community sessions.
- **2c deep scrape?**: NO. It's a content library, not a structured drill program; exercises are sparse and gated. Skip.

## Kindra Hall — Stories That Stick courses
- **Methodology**: Business-storytelling curriculum built on the 4-story framework (Value, Founder, Purpose, Customer) + Stellar Storytelling Method (identifiable character, authentic emotion, a moment, specific details) and a 3-stage Find/Craft/Tell process. Improvement = framework + guided worksheets + story-mining prompts.
- **Signature exercises**: 4 DIY Story Builder worksheets (Value/Customer/Founder/Purpose), 36 Story Prompts, 4-Minute Story Makeover critiques, Stellar four-component drill, Find/Craft/Tell walkthrough (Deep Dive).
- **Exercise count found**: 8 distinct activities.
- **Evidence quality**: Mostly confirmed — the Mastery sales page enumerates modules, video counts, worksheet counts, and named tools directly.
- **Format/pricing**: Self-paced. Mastery = $395 (or 3×$149), ~6 hrs video + workbook, lifetime access; Deep Dive is a separate book-implementation tier.
- **2c deep scrape?**: MEDIUM-YES. Most structured of the storytelling courses here, with clearly named per-story builder exercises worth modeling. The sales page already exposes the full module/worksheet list; deeper scrape only needed if we want the actual worksheet prompts. Start URL: https://courses.kindrahall.com/sts-mastery

## Mindvalley — Speak & Inspire (Lisa Nichols)
- **Methodology**: 31-day daily-quest format (~10 min/day video + task), heart/emotion-first speaking. Improvement = daily micro-lesson + small task + community accountability, bookended by pre/post self-assessment. Strong product pattern for a daily-habit app.
- **Signature exercises**: Verbal Highlights, Pregnant Pause, Valley Technique (low-point + lesson story), Care-frontation, Shaping a Story / vivid imagery, daily 10-min lesson+task loop, host-an-event capstone speech.
- **Exercise count found**: 8 activities.
- **Evidence quality**: Mostly confirmed — named techniques documented in detailed third-party reviews + Mindvalley product page. Reviews note exercises sometimes lack step-by-step demonstrations.
- **Format/pricing**: Mindvalley subscription ($99/mo or $499/yr; 15-day trial). Community feed for sharing/feedback.
- **2c deep scrape?**: MEDIUM-YES (for the FORMAT, not content). The quest/daily-task structure is the most app-aligned pattern in this batch and worth a deeper look at how lessons map to daily tasks; content is gated behind subscription. Start URL: https://www.mindvalley.com/speak

## Coursera — Introduction to Public Speaking (UW, Matt McGarrity)
- **Methodology**: Classic university MOOC, rhetoric-based. Improvement = design + record + peer-review real speeches across 5 weeks, with explicit rubrics. The most rep-and-feedback-driven course in the batch (recorded speeches + structured peer review).
- **Signature exercises**: Introductory speech, two-point impromptu speech, two speech-analysis assignments, elevator speech, Key Point Speech capstone, Q&A / difficult-situation / pause-length drills. (Some VR delivery experiences included.)
- **Exercise count found**: 6 distinct graded activities.
- **Evidence quality**: Confirmed — full weekly assignment list with time estimates pulled directly from the Coursera course page.
- **Format/pricing**: Freemium (audit free; cert paid). Peer review requires recording speeches on phone/camera.
- **2c deep scrape?**: YES. Highest-quality public rubrics and a clean recorded-speech + peer-review loop directly analogous to our recorded-exercise model. Worth scraping the assignment rubrics. Start URL: https://www.coursera.org/learn/public-speaking

## Improve Your Social Skills (Daniel Wendler)
- **Methodology**: Persona-aligned (socially anxious / autistic learners) step-by-step guide + paid video courses. Theory = treat social skills like learning a language: study the mechanics (body language, conversation flow), then deliberately practice one skill at a time in the field. Strong fit for our Anxious Introvert persona.
- **Signature exercises**: Muted-video body-language study, conversation-flow practice via free information, pick-one-skill daily practice, conversational story craft, meeting-people/making-friends sequence, dating-chapter application.
- **Exercise count found**: 6 activities.
- **Evidence quality**: Mixed-confirmed — chapter structure and several practice methods confirmed via the free guide + book reviews; the paid video-course internals and dating specifics are inferred.
- **Format/pricing**: Freemium — free web guide + book; paid intensive video courses (conversation, body language, empathy).
- **2c deep scrape?**: YES. The free guide is fully readable (no paywall on core chapters), persona-aligned, and structured enough to mine concrete exercises. Start URL: https://www.improveyoursocialskills.com/start-here

## Conversation Skills Core (Paul Sanders)
- **Methodology**: Conversation training for shy adults via free email/web 101 course funneling to paid programs. Improvement = simple repeatable micro-process (open → root in → thread on free information) practiced in everyday low-stakes settings. Low-cognitive-load by design.
- **Signature exercises**: 3-part How-to-Start-a-Conversation process, Rooting (smoothing in), free-information/hooks-and-threads practice, what-to-talk-about topic generation, the free 101 email course itself.
- **Exercise count found**: 5 activities.
- **Evidence quality**: Mostly confirmed — lesson titles and named techniques (rooting, free information) visible across the public tutorials and 101 course pages.
- **Format/pricing**: Freemium — free 101 email course + tutorials; paid 'Get the Friends You Want'-style programs.
- **2c deep scrape?**: MEDIUM-YES. Tutorials are public and the conversation-threading mechanics are concrete and persona-aligned; worth scraping the free tutorial set for exercise detail. Start URL: https://conversation-skills-core.com/conversation-skills-tutorials/
