## Improvement Pill
- Methodology: free YouTube "courses" (video series) + one paid Skillshare class. Theory of improvement = teach a concept per video, then have the viewer apply it in real life / record a project. Reps come from self-directed practice, not in-app feedback.
- Exercises found: 7. Strongest structured assets are the **Art of Storytelling** Skillshare class (two concrete record-your-own-story projects) and the **BeeFriend Course** (Formula of Friendship + "golden rule" likeability lessons). Tamed Course is habit-building, only adjacent to social skills.
- Evidence quality: mostly confirmed for the named lessons/projects (Skillshare syllabus, BeeFriend playlist + recaps). The Tamed Course assignments are inferred.
- Domains: storytelling, conversation, dating-social; some voice-tonality/oratory in the storytelling delivery lesson.
- 2c deep scrape warranted? **Yes, lightweight.** The Art of Storytelling Skillshare class has a clean lesson list and two reusable "record + self-assess a personal story" exercise patterns that map directly to riffy's recorded-speech format. Start URL: https://www.skillshare.com/en/classes/the-art-of-storytelling-how-to-craft-engaging-persuasive-and-memorable-stories/781588018 (plus the BeeFriend playlist https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLE_vQWWxgaiFitUPE6u4YP08qgY9n7ILo).

## Matthew Hussey — Get The Guy / Love Life Club
- Methodology: content + script-pack model with a live-coaching membership on top. Theory = give word-for-word customizable scripts/texts for specific moments, plus mindset/confidence video training and live Q&A reps in the Love Life Club. Female audience (counterweight to male-PUA seeds).
- Exercises found: 7. The "exercises" are mostly **script/text packs** (How to Talk to Men = 59 scripts; Momentum Texts = 67 texts; Get Him Running Back = 5-step scripts) plus journaling/gratitude (At-Home Retreat) and live Q&A coaching.
- Evidence quality: mostly confirmed from the official products page and program landing pages.
- Domains: dating-social, conversation; little voice/improv work.
- 2c deep scrape warranted? **No (low priority).** It is script-pack + live-coaching, not a drill catalog; the "what to say" scripts are useful as conversation-prompt inspiration but are gated/paid and not exercise-shaped. If revisited, start at https://matthewhussey.com/products/ to inventory the script packs only.

Strong "reps + feedback + real-world mission" loop with progressive topics (apps -> approaching -> conversation -> messaging -> flirting -> dates). Real Academy tier adds in-person IRL approach practice.
- Exercises found: 9 (6 weekly challenges + 2 makeovers + live coaching; Real Academy IRL inferred).
- Evidence quality: confirmed — full week-by-week breakdown pulled from the masterclass page.
- Domains: dating-social, conversation; non-sleazy/ethical positioning (touch, consent, timing).
- 2c deep scrape warranted? **Yes.** Best-structured weekly-challenge curriculum in the batch; the progressive field-assignment + feedback loop is a strong template for riffy's challenge/field-assignment formats. Start URL: https://www.hayleyquinn.com/men/masterclass (also women's masterclass https://www.hayleyquinn.com/women/masterclass and Real Academy https://www.hayleyquinn.com/men/academy).

## Introverted Alpha (Sarah Jones)
- Methodology: authenticity-first, "logical and sequential" coaching for analytical/introverted men, explicitly anti-PUA. Theory = draw out the client's genuine attractive strengths via structured 12-week module sequences + weekly 1:1 coaching, plus self-study programs with named step sequences. Strong persona match (introverted men).
- Exercises found: 6. Notable named assets: free ebook **3-step "uniquely attractive" written exercise**, **Find Your Alpha Archetype** quiz, **First Touch To First Kiss** 10-step sequence, Magnetic Confidence self-study, and two $4,997 12-week 1:1 programs.
- Evidence quality: confirmed for program structure, pricing, and the named exercises/quizzes; the inside-the-module assignment specifics are not public (high-ticket gated).
- Domains: dating-social, conversation.
- 2c deep scrape warranted? **Yes, lightweight.** The free ebook (3-step exercise) and the alpha-archetype quiz are concrete, scrapeable, and persona-aligned; the 10-step "First Touch To First Kiss" sequence is a reusable progression model. Start URL: https://introvertedalpha.com/ebook/ (plus https://introvertedalpha.com/alpha-archetype/ and https://introvertedalpha.com/programs/). 1:1 program internals are gated and not worth chasing.

## Logan Murray — Comedy Course (UK)
- Methodology: the most exercise-led teacher in the batch. Live London stand-up course (with Amused Moose, "Stand Up and Deliver"), exercise-driven: performance/improv exercises for spontaneity and finding your comic persona, generative writing games to build "comedic grammar," weekly homework with group feedback, solo material critiqued in detail, ending in a 5-minute public showcase. His book "Be a Great Stand-up" is essentially a catalog of these writing games and improv drills (incl. the 'rant' exercise, positive-then-negative tagging).
- Exercises found: 7 (and the book holds many more individually-namable drills — under-counted here).
- Evidence quality: confirmed for the exercise *types* and showcase structure from course pages, Amused Moose, and book reviews; specific course duration/price not retrievable (site intermittently down) and individual book drills are partly inferred.
- Domains: humor-jokes, improv, quick-wit, oratory.
- 2c deep scrape warranted? **Yes — highest priority for exercise mining.** The book is a dense source of solo-friendly generative writing games and improv drills directly applicable to riffy's humor/improv/quick-wit drill formats. Start by sourcing the book "Be a Great Stand-up" (Teach Yourself) and scraping its exercise list; secondary URL https://loganmurray.com/stand-up-course (site was intermittently 403/refusing — retry).
