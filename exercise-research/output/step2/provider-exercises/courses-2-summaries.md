# Step 2b Provider Summaries — Batch: courses-2

Batch type: online courses / coaches (conversation, voice, humor, social-power).
Researched 2026-06-10. Evidence drawn from public blog articles, course landing/sales pages, third-party reviews, and download-site curriculum mirrors. Udemy and Skillshare product pages were anti-scrape (403), so those entries lean on search snippets + the providers' own descriptions.

---

## SocialSelf (David Morin)
- Methodology: SEO-scale social-skills publication; theory of improvement = teach a named, repeatable technique per problem (threading, IFR, FORD, etc.) backed by psychology, then push graded real-world exposure ("daily low-stakes reps" up to harder approaches). Strongly product-shaped: each article = a drill.
- Exercises found: 13 distinct, all confirmed directly from the blog (Conversational Threading, IFR Method, FORD, Map Method, Five Senses, Environmental Opener, Question Progression, Paraphrasing, Group Signaling, Mission Trick, Daily Practice, Speed Reduction, Lead-by-Example Texting).
- Evidence quality: mostly confirmed (read directly off article text).
- 2c warranted: YES — the blog is a goldmine of named conversation drills with step-by-step instructions, and it's also a direct SEO competitor. Start scrape from https://socialself.com/blog/ (sitemap) — high yield, low effort, public.

## SocialSelf — Conversation Confidence program
- Methodology: 5-chapter / 9-"day" self-paced course, ~5–10 min/day of video+exercise, with built-in conversation simulations to rehearse from home; explicit "real change happens in real interactions" framing (tiny daily dose + exposure). Distinct product from the free blog.
- Exercises found: 6 confirmed (simulations + named day-by-day methods: bypass self-consciousness, leverage others' words, overcome silence, Attentional Shift, Check-In, permanent results).
- Evidence quality: confirmed at the day/title level; the actual simulation scripts and exercise mechanics are behind the paywall (not visible).
- 2c warranted: YES (medium) — the day-by-day skeleton + "conversation simulation" format is the most app-like thing in this batch and worth a deeper scrape/sample. Start from https://socialself.com/conversation-confidence-rp/ ; need a purchase or a detailed third-party walkthrough to see exercise internals.

## Roger Love — The Perfect Voice / Voice of Influence
- Methodology: celebrity vocal coach; theory = your speaking voice is trainable like a singing voice. Anatomy first, then diagnose voice 'types' (airy/monotone/nasal), then fundamentals (breathing, chest/middle/head voice, pitch/pace/tone/melody/volume), then application via 'Vocal Profiles' (per-scenario). Format = audio/video drill reps + daily warm-ups, record yourself.
- Exercises found: 12 confirmed named drills (Slow Leak breathing, Gog scale, Middle Voice, Daily Warm-Ups, Melody/Pitch, Volume air-cord balance, Happy Birthday, Newscaster Method, Emotional Mapping 4-step, Vocal Profiles, Um/Uh elimination, Balloon Trick).
- Evidence quality: mostly confirmed — named exercises appear in his learning center, sales pages, and reviews; exact mechanics of paid drills partly inferred.
- 2c warranted: YES (high) — best-documented technique taxonomy in the batch and directly maps to a voice-tonality exercise track. The Perfect Voice ($97–$147; 7 sections, 45+ videos) is the cleanest structured program to deep-scrape. Start from https://bathroomsingingcrew.com/roger-love-perfect-voice/ (module list) + https://rogerlove.com/learning-center-v2/.

## Udemy charisma/communication catalog (topic page)
- Methodology: this is a marketplace, not one method — dozens of independent courses (charisma, banter, small talk, humor writing, improv-for-anxiety). Common shapes: multi-day action challenges (21X Charisma = 21 daily exercises), 5-step conversation systems, starter/scenario banks, solo improv warm-ups, joke-writing write-alongs. Public course outlines make it cheap curriculum-mining across every domain.
- Exercises found: 6 representative exercise *types* (21-day daily challenge, 5-step conversation system, conversation-starter bank, solo improv warm-ups, body-language drills, comedy-writing exercises); 4 confirmed from course descriptions, 2 inferred.
- Evidence quality: mixed — course existence/structure confirmed via search; exact lecture-level exercises behind Udemy 403 wall (mostly inferred from 'what you'll learn' blurbs).
- 2c warranted: PARTIAL/NO as a single scrape (it's not one program). Better to cherry-pick 2–3 specific high-rated courses (e.g. 'Next Level Conversation', '21X Charisma') and deep-dive those individually. If doing one, start from https://www.udemy.com/topic/charisma/ but expect to need Class Central / Comidoc mirrors for curricula.

## Killer Stand-up (Steve Roye)
- Methodology: contrarian stand-up system since 2001 — explicitly rejects 'conventional joke writing' (set-up/punchline formulas, stage characters). 8 core modules / 28 lessons / ~556 pages across a Writing Guide + Performing Guide, plus 'Comedy Evaluator Pro' software to objectively score how funny material is from audience-laughter data. Theory: develop material from authentic POV, test empirically, then drill delivery.
- Exercises found: 4 (Comedy Mindset reset, Non-joke-writing material development, Comedy Evaluator Pro testing, Performing/delivery drills) — 3 confirmed, 1 inferred. The free Module 1 lessons are mostly mindset/de-programming, not drills.
- Evidence quality: structure confirmed; actual exercise mechanics largely behind paywall (the 5 free lessons reveal philosophy, not activities).
- 2c warranted: NO (low priority) — old-school, humor-jokes-only, and the most interesting asset (Comedy Evaluator Pro / measurement loop) is the concept rather than scrapeable exercises. Note the *measurement* idea for product inspiration. If revisited, start from https://www.killerstandup.com/.

## Online Comedy Training
- Methodology: self-paced beginner→advanced stand-up *writing* progression (7+ hrs video). Theory: humor has 'one unbreakable law' + 3 core elements; teach a 4-step joke method, then 12 one-liner joke types each with theory→steps→pro examples→deconstruction→random-word walkthrough, then 'organic' conversion of experiences/observations/opinions, then comedic storytelling. Very curriculum/progression-shaped — maps cleanly to a leveled exercise track.
- Exercises found: 6 confirmed (Mechanics 'explain every joke', 4-step method, 12 joke-type walkthroughs, random-word prompt drill, organic-conversion exercises, comedic-storytelling build).
- Evidence quality: mostly confirmed — module list and exercise formats are spelled out on the public sales page.
- 2c warranted: YES (medium-high for humor-jokes domain) — the clearest 'write-along, leveled' humor curriculum in the batch and the random-word-prompt drill is directly app-able. Start from https://onlinecomedytraining.com/ (and /store).

## Skillshare — Level Up Your Humor (Felipe Yanez)
- Methodology: short (~51 min) conversational-comedy class. 4 sections: Humor Mindset (inner game) → Humor Styles (find your style w/ comedian examples) → Techniques & Conversational Comedy Structure (banter, illustrated w/ talk-show clips) → Exercises, capped by a 30-Day Humor Challenge (say something funny in one conversation/day). Theory: humor is a trainable conversational skill + daily field reps.
- Exercises found: 4 confirmed (Humor Mindset, Humor Styles ID, Banter Techniques, 30-Day Humor Challenge).
- Evidence quality: confirmed at section level via course descriptions; lecture-level detail behind Skillshare/Udemy 403.
- 2c warranted: NO (low) — short and shallow; main takeaway is the 30-Day-Challenge format (one funny line/day) which is a clean app mechanic to borrow. No deep scrape needed; the structure is already captured.

## The Power Moves — Power University
- Methodology: sociologist Lucio Buffalmano's research-synthesis course on power dynamics / advanced social skills. 8 modules (+2 bonus) basics→mastery: power bedrock, status/respect, attraction/dating, relationship leadership, dark-triad edge, business/negotiation, execution tools. Learning assets: real-life video & text breakdowns, high-power-voice audio drills, dominance-showdown calibration, self-assessments, quizzes, case studies, assignments, community real-life scenarios. Pricey (€797–€9,979). The site ALSO hosts detailed reviews of competitor courses — useful meta-source.
- Exercises found: 6 confirmed exercise families (high-power voice drills, text/video breakdowns, micro-aggression/taunt calibration, self-assessments/quizzes, assignments + community scenarios, Machiavellian negotiation scripts).
- Evidence quality: confirmed at module/asset level; concrete drill scripts behind a steep paywall.
- 2c warranted: PARTIAL — skip the (expensive, gated) course internals, but DO mine the free thepowermoves.com competitor-review articles as a meta-source for other providers, and note the micro-aggression-comeback / dominance-showdown drills as a quick-wit exercise pattern. Start from https://thepowermoves.com/ (blog/reviews), not the paid course.
