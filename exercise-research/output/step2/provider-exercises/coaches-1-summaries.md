# Step 2b Provider Summaries — Batch: coaches-1

## Matthew Dicks — Storyworthy / Storyworthy Workshops
- **Methodology**: Story-first, not joke-first. Improvement comes from (a) constant raw-material capture (Homework for Life), (b) generative free-writing (Crash & Burn, First/Last/Best/Worst), and (c) a tight kit of structural/suspense devices applied to true 5-minute first-person stories, refined via live Zoom workshops (Moth/StorySLAM lineage).
- **Exercises found**: 12 — 3 discovery/free-writing drills, ~7 named structure/suspense devices (Five-Second Moment, Elephant, Backpack, Hourglass, Crystal Ball, But/Therefore, Planting Bombs, Opening in Opposition), plus live workshop reps.
- **Evidence quality**: Mostly confirmed. The discovery exercises and devices are documented directly in the book, his site, and many independent book-summary notes. Live-workshop specifics are inferred from format descriptions.
- **2c deep scrape**: YES — richest named-exercise source in storytelling. Start from his book-summary corpus and https://www.storyworthymd.com/storyworthy-moments ; consider mining a full chapter-by-chapter summary (e.g. steve-anderson.co/notes/storyworthy, aliabdaal.com) for any remaining devices (e.g. "Babies & Blenders", "Milk Cans & a Baseball", marginalization, scarcity-of-characters) not yet itemized.

## Todd V Dating — The System
- **Methodology**: Structured, fundamentals-first verbal-game curriculum built on a 5-step conversation arc (Open -> Premise/Judge Frame -> Evaluate -> Narrative -> Close), heavy on infield video analysis (8+ hrs) and modeling, with solo rehearsal ("The Wall"), isolation drills (Verbal Game Academy), and real-world field reports in higher tiers. Reps + analysis + field application.
- **Exercises found**: 9 — solo rehearsal drill, opener-type/premise/evaluate/narrative/close drills, infield breakdowns, field reports, and VGA isolation drills (openers, push-pull, emotional spikes, shit-test deflection).
- **Evidence quality**: Mostly confirmed. Framework and "The Wall" come from a detailed third-party review (Power Moves) and official sales pages; VGA week structure confirmed from verbalgameacademy.com. Field-report assignments are inferred (standard for bootcamp/mentorship tiers).
- **2c deep scrape**: YES (with caveat) — the Verbal Game Academy 8-week module map (Opening, Attraction 1/2, Evaluation, Frame Control, Narrative, Sexual Tension, Her Blueprint) and toddvdating.com category pages are the most exercise-dense. Start from https://verbalgameacademy.com/ and https://toddvdating.com/category/verbal-game-academy/ . Domain is dating-specific (single domain), so prioritize only if dating-social is in scope.

## Greg Dean — Stand-Up Comedy Classes
- **Methodology**: The most codified joke-mechanics system in the batch. Improvement = learn explicit joke anatomy (Setup -> Assumptions -> Connector -> Reinterpretation -> Punchline), then generate on demand with the two-part Joke Prospector (Joke Map for setups, Joke Mine for punchlines), then add performance layers (act-outs, POVs, crowd work) and rehearsal psychology (Critic Split). Structured workbooks turn each into reps.
- **Exercises found**: 10 — 3 writing systems/drills (Joke Map, Joke Mine, mechanism analysis), ranting, act-outs/3 POVs, crowd work, writing-group/tagging, Critic Split, mic technique, plus the 5-workbook exercise series.
- **Evidence quality**: Mostly confirmed. Mechanisms and Joke Prospector steps are spelled out on his own site and online-course outline; workbook contents confirmed via listings.
- **2c deep scrape**: YES — highest density of discrete, teachable mechanics for humor-jokes. Start from https://stand-upcomedy.com/joke-writing-made-simple/ and the academy course outline https://www.gregdeancomedyacademy.com/p/stand-up-comedy-techniques-performance-and-writing ; the "7 Instant Joke Writing Techniques" mini-course and glossary (stand-upcomedy.com/glossary/) likely yield more named techniques.

## The Art of Charm
- **Methodology**: Social-skills/confidence system delivered three ways — free podcast "Toolbox" episodes (each a self-contained named drill), a 5-day residential bootcamp (role-plays + 1-on-1 coaching + real-world missions), and the online X-Factor Accelerator (8 named frameworks + weekly Language Labs + AI roleplay coach + peer cohort). Reps via role-play, live feedback, and field missions.
- **Exercises found**: 10 — vocal tonality, opening/cold approach, five fundamentals (eye contact/body language), rapport, narrative building, social-capital networking, bootcamp role-plays, field missions, mindset reflective work, and the X-Factor 8-framework reps incl. an AI roleplay coach.
- **Evidence quality**: Mostly confirmed at the title/topic level (the Toolbox episode pages and bootcamp/X-Factor pages name each drill); the granular step-by-step of a few drills (exact vocal exercises) is inferred since full content lives in the audio/transcripts.
- **2c deep scrape**: YES — the Toolbox episode archive is a large, free, named-drill library spanning conversation, voice-tonality, storytelling, and dating-social. Start from https://theartofcharm.com/category/podcast-episodes/toolbox-episodes/ and mine individual episode transcripts (each has a downloadable PDF) for concrete drill steps; also https://join.theartofcharm.com/xfa for the AI-roleplay framework set.

## Judy Carter — Comedy Bible workshops
- **Methodology**: Problem-to-punchline pipeline. Improvement = systematic, day-by-day workbook exercises that mine personal pain/anger for material, then apply joke structure (setup/punchline/act-out, list-of-three, callbacks), define a persona/POV, and polish a set live. Strong for both comics and professionals adding humor ("Punching Up").
- **Exercises found**: 10 — the 48 Exercises set, 30 Morning Writings prompts, authentic-topic mind-map, act-outs, rule of three, killer opening + callback closer, persona/POV, crowd work/heckler handling, Punching Up, and live/online set polishing.
- **Evidence quality**: Mixed-to-confirmed. The workbook's five-section structure and headline exercises (48 exercises, 30 prompts, act-out, rule of three, callbacks) are confirmed via the store page, Amazon, and book PDFs; the full itemized list of all 48 individual exercises is not publicly enumerated (so a handful are inferred). Crowd work/heckler handling is inferred.
- **2c deep scrape**: YES — the full 48-exercise list plus 30 Morning Writings is the prize but is paywalled. Start from the New Comedy Bible PDF (https://www.scribd.com/document/696327973/) and the workbook store page (https://www.judycarter.com/store-IEaE2/p/the-comedy-bible-workbook-download); a 2c pass would need the actual book/workbook to enumerate each of the 48 exercises by name.
