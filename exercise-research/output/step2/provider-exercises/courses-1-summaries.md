# Step 2b Provider Summaries — Batch: courses-1

Batch of 8 structured course/book providers (oratory/voice, humor, conversation/social). Researched via web search, course pages, reviews, walkthroughs, and book summaries.

---

## Vinh Giang — STAGE Academy
- **Methodology:** Reps + structured curriculum (7 modules, 120+ short 5-15 min videos) anchored on the "5 vocal foundations" (rate, volume, pitch, tonality, pause), plus storytelling, body language, and self-awareness. Core practice loop = record yourself on phone (audio/video/transcript), review against the foundations, re-record with adjustments.
- **Exercises found:** 11 (rate drill, volume 1-10 scale, siren/pitch, tongue twisters, pause, tonality+emotion, vocal archetypes, record-review loop, storybank storytelling, body-language/presence, rapport technique).
- **Evidence:** Mostly confirmed. The 5 foundations and named drills (siren, tongue twisters, pause, volume scale) are well-documented in his free YouTube content and reviews; the exact in-course exercise wording behind the paywall is inferred. "Powerful Rapport Technique" is inferred (name only).
- **2c warranted?** YES. Strong structured program, highest-value oratory/voice source in batch. The paid Kajabi course is paywalled, but his YouTube channel (@askvinh) and the UNSTOPPABLE "5 Vocal Foundations" video demonstrate the actual drills. Start a 2c deep scrape from https://www.youtube.com/@askvinh and the 5-vocal-foundations video (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CuaY4qe4V34) for exact drill mechanics; supplement with karpfucius.com review for module structure.

## Gotham Writers Workshop — Stand-Up Comedy Writing
- **Methodology:** Structured 10-week workshop (lectures + weekly writing exercises + peer/instructor critique), building from joke analysis → POV → editing → audience → persona → assembling and rehearsing a 5-minute set, ending in an optional live club performance. Classic write-perform-revise loop.
- **Exercises found:** 7 (weekly: joke analysis, POV I, improve-a-joke editing/dissection, writing for the audience, persona+timing, build/rehearse 5-min set, workshop critique).
- **Evidence:** Confirmed. Public syllabus on writingclasses.com + CourseHorse gives week-by-week topics; the exact in-class exercise handouts are inferred from topic names.
- **2c warranted?** PARTIAL / LOW. Syllabus is public and already extracted at week granularity; little additional structured detail behind the (paid, instructor-led) class. Skip deep scrape unless you want the exact weekly assignment prompts — if so, start from https://wp.writingclasses.com/courses/stand-up-comedy-writing/ (note: returns 403 to bots; use CourseHorse mirror).

## Udemy — Ultimate Joke Writing Guide
- **Methodology:** Self-paced video course teaching 12 named joke structures (Broken Assumption, Exaggeration, Understatement, Specificity, Contradiction, Pun, Meta/Anti-comedy, Comparison, Contrast, Analogy, Callback, Parody) with joke psychology + step-by-step write-along walkthroughs and comedian compilation analysis. Pure structure-based reps.
- **Exercises found:** 7 (one per structure cluster, plus random-word write-along and compilation analysis).
- **Evidence:** Confirmed. The 12 structures are listed publicly; the per-lecture write-along format is confirmed via course description.
- **2c warranted?** YES (high value, easy). This is the cleanest taxonomy of joke mechanics in the batch and maps directly to writing-prompt exercises for an app. The full structure list + theory is extractable from the public Udemy curriculum without purchase. Start from https://www.udemy.com/course/ultimate-joke-writing-guide-for-writing-stand-up-comedy-jokes/ (curriculum tab) and Jared Volle's free joke-structure guide PDF (https://creativestandup.com/wp-content/uploads/manual/joke-structure-guide.pdf).

## Udemy — How to Be Funny in 14 Days
- **Methodology:** Pattern-extraction from 1,000+ talk-show clips → distilled into copy-able tactics and exact lines for everyday conversational wit (not stage comedy). Explicitly "100% actionable tactics, no mindset fluff." Reps over 14 days.
- **Exercises found:** 6 (easiest-way borrowed line, humorous compliment, teasing/responding to teasing, deflection lines for awkward questions, funny on stage, talk-show pattern extraction).
- **Evidence:** Mostly confirmed at the topic level (curriculum bullets are public); the exact lines/scripts are behind the paywall (inferred).
- **2c warranted?** YES. Highly aligned to riffy's conversational/quick-wit domain (this is everyday banter, not stand-up). Worth a deeper scrape of the public curriculum + reviews to extract the specific tactic categories. Start from https://www.udemy.com/course/how-to-be-funny-in-14-days/ (curriculum tab) and the tutorialspoint mirror.

## A Funnier You (David Nihill ecosystem)
- **Methodology:** Humor-for-speakers, "5 minutes a day" daily practice habit framed around David Nihill's 7 Comedy Habits (Start with a Story, Find the Funny, Write Funny, Rehearsed Spontaneity, Delivery, Control the Audience, Permanent Beta). NOTE: afunnieryou.com itself currently resolves to Rick Olson's "Hook 'Em With Humor" sales page (Humor Toolkit / Laugh Generator / 90-Day Humor Plan) — the step-2a "David Nihill ecosystem" attribution looks stale; the substantive exercise content is in Nihill's book *Do You Talk Funny?*.
- **Exercises found:** 6 (funniest-moments inventory, start-with-a-story, rehearsed spontaneity, write-funny tightening, delivery/timing, 5-min-a-day habit).
- **Evidence:** Mostly inferred. The 7-habits framework is confirmed from the book; the specific per-exercise mechanics and the afunnieryou.com daily drills were not directly visible (sales-page only).
- **2c warranted?** PARTIAL. Provider attribution is muddy. If pursued, deep-dive the actual source: David Nihill's *Do You Talk Funny?* book (AnyFlip preview https://anyflip.com/cisq/skso/basic/51-100) for the exercises, NOT the afunnieryou.com landing page. Lower priority than the Udemy/Vinh sources.

## Charisma on Command — Charisma University
- **Methodology:** 30-day / 6-week behavior-change program. 6 video modules (First Impressions, Confidence, Expert Conversation, Storytelling, Presence & Magnetism, + bonus). The defining mechanic is a daily action guide: short action video → one concrete real-world action step (2-20 min) every weekday, with a forum for feedback. Habit-formation via daily field assignments.
- **Exercises found:** 6 (4-emotions first impression, confidence inner-game, expert-conversation never-run-out, storytelling everyday-events, presence/magnetism nonverbals, daily-action-guide mechanic).
- **Evidence:** Confirmed at module + mechanic level (public sales page + reviews + a public course-notes site charismaoncommand.university). Exact daily action wording mostly inferred.
- **2c warranted?** YES. Best example in batch of a daily-field-assignment structure (very relevant to a daily-practice app). A public day-by-day course-notes mirror exists. Start 2c from https://charismaoncommand.university/lds-course-notes/ (day-by-day notes, e.g. "Day 11 – Intro to Expert Conversation") to extract the actual daily action steps.

## Science of People — People School (Vanessa Van Edwards)
- **Methodology:** Year-long, research-backed 12-skill curriculum (10-20 hrs) with video skills → 3 real-world activation challenges per skill → 12 coach-reviewed assignments + 150+ pages of worksheets. Named skills include Charisma (warmth/competence), Be Engaging (3 gaze types), Asserting Yourself (lead vs follow), Wield Influence (winner body language). Reps + worksheets + accountability.
- **Exercises found:** 9 (charisma balance, three gaze types, lead/follow, winner body-language, first-impressions/rapport, per-skill 3-challenge mechanic, worksheets, free-blog "how to be funny" steps).
- **Evidence:** Mixed. The per-skill challenge mechanic and worksheets are confirmed; only ~4-5 of the 12 module names are documented in public reviews (rest inferred). The free blog exercises are confirmed and directly extractable.
- **2c warranted?** YES — but target the FREE blog, not the $1.5k course. scienceofpeople.com has an enormous library of self-contained, exercise-rich articles (how to be funny, conversation starters, charisma, body language) that are the easiest high-quality extraction in the batch. Start 2c from https://www.scienceofpeople.com/ blog index rather than the paywalled People School curriculum.

## Patrick King (books: The Art of Witty Banter, Improve Your Conversations)
- **Methodology:** Conversation-coach author with 30+ books of named, actionable techniques for wit and conversation. No course — pure book frameworks the reader drills: HPM, SBR, EDR decision-trees for what to say next; free-association for spontaneity; banter moves (agree-and-amplify, fourth-wall break, teasing/comebacks, 2-second rule).
- **Exercises found:** 8 (free association, HPM, SBR, EDR, 2-second rule, fourth-wall break, teasing/comebacks, agree-and-amplify/misattribution/us-against-the-world).
- **Evidence:** Mostly confirmed. HPM/SBR/EDR + free association + 2-second rule + fourth-wall break are confirmed directly from the book PDF/summary; the later banter chapters (agree-and-amplify etc.) are inferred from chapter summaries.
- **2c warranted?** YES — highest priority for quick-wit domain. These are the most directly app-mappable named drills in the batch (each is a discrete, repeatable conversational move). Start 2c from the full book text: Internet Archive (https://archive.org/details/artofwittybanter0000king) and the Bookey PDF summary; also mine *Improve Your Conversations* (the HPM/EDR source) and his improv-for-conversation title.
