# Step 2b Provider Summaries — Batch apps-3

## SmartyMe
- **Methodology:** Content microlearning, not drill/practice. Theory of improvement = daily exposure to expert tips (read or listen) builds skills over time. No interactive speaking practice; user consumes 15-min lessons and works through multi-day "challenges" that bundle lessons into goal tracks.
- **Exercises found:** 3 distinct activity types (daily lesson, audio lesson, self-growth challenges). Effectively one core unit (the lesson) in 3 delivery wrappers.
- **Evidence quality:** Confirmed via official help center + app store listings. Internal lesson list (exact module names within Public Speaking / Communication / Voice) not surfaced.
- **2c recommendation:** **No.** It's passive content, not exercises; low relevance to a drill/practice product. Skip unless we want to mine their topic taxonomy. If revisited, start at https://smartyme.zendesk.com/hc and the Communication/Public Speaking category lists.

## Poised
- **Methodology:** Feedback-in-context. Improve by getting live, objective metrics during real meetings, then reflecting on a scored recap; expert lessons are secondary. Learning loop = real call → live nudges → post-meeting Poised score → coaching plan. A standalone practice mode exists but is lightly documented.
- **Exercises found:** 4 (real-time feedback, post-meeting recap/score, in-app practice mode, expert lessons/coaching plan). Only ~1 is a deliberate "practice activity"; the rest are feedback on real performance.
- **Evidence quality:** Mostly confirmed (homepage + AppSumo + review sites). The practice mode is inferred/under-documented.
- **2c recommendation:** **No (low priority).** Almost no structured exercise catalog to scrape — value is the metric taxonomy (filler words, hedging, speakshare, inclusive language, pausing), which is already captured. If revisited, start at https://www.poised.com/ and any in-app "Learn" tab.

## BoldVoice
- **Methodology:** Strongest drill-loop design in the batch. Assessment → personalized path → daily video lesson (coach demos a sound) → immediate repeat-after-me drill with color-coded AI scoring → repeat until correct. Accent domain (adjacent), but the lesson+practice loop, gamified scoring, and AI roleplay are directly instructive for our product.
- **Exercises found:** 6 well-defined, clearly differentiated exercise types (SpeechScan, video lessons, Challenge Words drill, AI Chat roleplay, Practice-Your-Text, Sound Library).
- **Evidence quality:** Confirmed — detailed official app guide enumerates each feature with what the user does.
- **2c recommendation:** **Yes.** Best structured-program example in the batch for drill design and the AI-feedback loop. Start a deep scrape at https://boldvoice.com/blog/boldvoice-app-guide plus the blog index https://boldvoice.com/blog and app-store screenshots.

## Astound — Voice & Speech Coach
- **Methodology:** Coach-authored vocal-training program built around daily warmups + a 30-day progressive challenge + scenario practice packs. Theory = short daily reps (breathing, resonance, projection, affirmations) with self-recording and visual feedback build calm/presence. Closest analog to a structured "exercise library" in this batch.
- **Exercises found:** 6 types (daily warmups, 100+ video tutorials, 30-Day Challenge, practice packs, recording w/ feedback, affirmations) — and importantly a 100+ exercise library behind them.
- **Evidence quality:** Confirmed at category level (app store + official site). The full enumerated list of the 100+ individual exercises is NOT yet captured.
- **2c recommendation:** **Yes.** A genuine structured program with a large named exercise catalog worth enumerating (and the practice-pack-per-occasion model is a strong pattern). Start at https://apps.apple.com/us/app/astound-voice-speech-coach/id1253443132 and http://www.astoundthem.com/; mine screenshots/walkthroughs for individual exercise names.

## TalkTune
- **Methodology:** AI roleplay + real-time voice analysis. Improve by repeatedly speaking through adaptive AI simulations (interviews, customer support, exam, open conversation) and acting on granular delivery feedback (pitch, empathy, confidence, pace, volume, clarity). Scenario-library driven; pivoted from interview-only toward general communication ("Fluent Me").
- **Exercises found:** 5 (role-specific interview sim, customer-support roleplay, conversational practice, speaking-test sim, real-time voice feedback). Several role tracks within the interview sim.
- **Evidence quality:** Confirmed — homepage and use-case pages enumerate role tracks and scenario types; exact full scenario count not stated.
- **2c recommendation:** **Yes (medium).** Worth a deeper pass to map the full scenario/persona library and the delivery-feedback rubric, both directly relevant to AI-roleplay design. Start at https://www.talktune.ai/ and the /use-cases/* pages.

## YourMove AI
- **Methodology:** Crutch / done-for-you generation, NOT training. The AI writes openers, replies, bios, and profile critiques for the user. No practice or skill-building loop. Value to us is purely the scenario taxonomy (opener types, reply contexts) and the 5-personality tone model (Funny/Flirty/Feisty/Friendly/Formal).
- **Exercises found:** 4 generation features (opener gen, reply/closer gen w/ tone, profile writer, profile review) — none are exercises in the practice sense.
- **Evidence quality:** Confirmed via homepage + multiple review sites.
- **2c recommendation:** **No.** No exercises to scrape; the useful taxonomy (tones + dating scenarios) is already captured here. Skip for 2c.

## Cues
- **Methodology:** Perception-side training (reading people), the inverse of expression-side wit. Gamified simulation engine: watch real-life video clips and decode nonverbal cues, with daily 5-min drills, micro-expression progression, rapid-fire genuine-vs-fake tests, and Social IQ scoring/ranking. Strong gamification model.
- **Exercises found:** 5 distinct, well-differentiated activity types (1,000+ video scenarios, daily drills, micro-expression analysis, Real-or-Fake tests, scenario-domain lessons).
- **Evidence quality:** Confirmed — app store listing enumerates the gamified modes and content volume.
- **2c recommendation:** **Yes (medium).** Excellent gamified-drill + daily-streak + scoring model to learn from, even though it's perception not expression. Start at https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/cues-learn-body-language/id1169108151 and https://learncues.com/; mine the curriculum/topic structure and the "Real or Fake?" mechanic.
