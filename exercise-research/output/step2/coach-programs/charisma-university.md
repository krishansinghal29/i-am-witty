## 1. Who the coach is

**Charisma on Command (COC)** is the brand founded by **Charlie Houpert** (with
co-founder Ben Altman). It is one of the largest self-improvement properties on
the internet, anchored by a **YouTube channel with ~6.6M+ subscribers** that
publishes "video analysis" breakdowns — they take a charismatic public figure
(Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, Emma Watson, Tony Robbins, Conan O'Brien, etc.)
and reverse-engineer the specific behaviors that make them magnetic, then turn
each into a teachable, practiceable tip. Houpert is also the author of the book
**_Charisma on Command_** (a separate 30-day plan — see the warning in §4).

**Charisma University** ("CU") is COC's **flagship paid program** — a structured,
**30-day / 6-week, action-based online course** that is the commercial endpoint of
the free YouTube funnel.

**Why it matters for this project:** CU is unusually well-suited to a play-it-out,
lower-the-stakes self-improvement app because it is **explicitly built as a
day-by-day habit installer, not a lecture library**. Every single day pairs a
short teaching video with a concrete **"Action Guide"** — a small field assignment
that takes "2–20 minutes" to do in real life (or solo at a desk). The official
positioning is literally: *"Put in 20 minutes a day for 30 days and you won't just
understand charisma — you'll embody it."* That maps almost 1:1 onto runnable daily
exercises. It also leans heavily on **inner-game / confidence work** (incantations,
sentence completions, visualization, values clarification, self-esteem), which is
directly on-target for an anxious-introvert audience, even though COC's house
*style* is high-energy extrovert (a noted limitation — see §5).

**Audience:** primarily younger men who want to be more socially magnetic at
parties, in dating, at work, and in leadership; broadly, anyone who wants to make
better first impressions, hold conversations, tell stories, and project presence.
Reviewers note the high-energy/extroverted framing fits "parties and fraternities"
better than quiet introverts — relevant when adapting the drills.

## 3. The methodology (theory of improvement)

CU's theory of change rests on a handful of load-bearing ideas:

1. **Charisma is a learnable skill, not an innate trait** — broken into observable,
   copyable behaviors. The whole brand thesis: you can reverse-engineer what
   magnetic people *do* and install it through practice.

2. **Action over information; habits over knowledge.** CU is "completely action
   based." The unit of the program is not a lecture but a **daily Action Guide** —
   a small, concrete, real-world (or desk) assignment. *"It is all about taking
   small, concrete steps every day so that after 6 weeks, you have made exuding
   charisma a habit."* Drill one thing at a time until it becomes unthinking.

3. **First impressions = hit the right emotions in the right order.** A great
   first impression isn't one thing; it's landing **positivity → trust → respect →
   genuine interest**, roughly in sequence (these overlap and unfold together).

4. **Confidence is generated, then made durable.** Short-term: "turn it on" via a
   Tony-Robbins-style **state-change** ritual (recall a peak moment → move your
   body the way you did then → say an **incantation**). Long-term: build
   *unshakeable* confidence on a base of **honesty, courage, living your values,
   and self-esteem** (so you're not just "whipping yourself into state").

5. **Conversation is about lowering your filter and going one level deeper.** Stop
   master-planning what to say; **speak with less filter**, make people laugh
   early to break the ice, then steer toward **values and emotions** (one level
   deeper than facts) so connection — not small talk — happens. Connection is a
   two-way street: share your own values, not just extract theirs. Intent matters
   more than technique.

6. **Storytelling = calibrate to the audience, don't race A→Z.** Stories aren't
   recited; they're *performed and adjusted live*. CU's structure: find the
   **overarching question/purpose** under the story → drop a **hook** to grab
   attention → tell it in **present tense** → **rising action** where everything
   said serves the point → **expand the climax / punchline** → **wrap up** with the
   moral. (Reviewers call this CU's strongest module.) Two story types: funny vs.
   purpose-driven.

7. **Presence/magnetism is physical and emotional.** **Open body language** has a
   measurable emotional impact; confident people move slowly and take up space
   ("a hook pulling you up" for posture). Add an **infectious smile**, living in
   the moment, brief **1–2 second touch** to bond, a **grounded, full-range voice**,
   and you become someone people are drawn to. Authenticity > seeking validation.

8. **Leadership = vision + understanding human motivation + conquering your own
   limiting beliefs.** Lead by example, craft a vision people want to follow,
   understand the core human drives, practice empathy, create win-win situations,
   and forgive yourself / stop over-analyzing.

In short: **reverse-engineer charismatic behaviors → install one small action per
day → start with first impressions and confidence, layer on conversation,
storytelling, presence, and leadership → make it a habit over 30 days.**

### 4c. The signature COC drills behind the Action Guides (method detail)

These are the concrete, practiceable techniques COC teaches (in the course and,
in disguised form, free on YouTube/the book). Where the *exact* Action-Guide
wording is gated, these are the public versions of the same drills:

- **State-change confidence ritual (Day 6):** (1) recall a moment you felt great,
  (2) **move your body** the way you did then, (3) repeat a punchy **incantation**
  to yourself. A fast "turn it on" routine. *(detail confirmed via Power Moves
  review.)*
- **Sentence completions (Days 6 & 10):** repeatedly finish an open-ended prompt
  many times to surface beliefs/values, then (Day 10) **find the patterns** across
  your answers to map your real values and self-esteem leaks. *(titles confirmed;
  format inferred from standard Robbins/Cabane-style sentence-completion drills.)*
- **Visualization (Day 8):** mentally rehearse an upcoming interaction going well,
  expecting to be noticed (3 named tips). *(title confirmed.)*
- **Sharing your truth / honesty in conversation (Day 9):** practice saying the
  honest, slightly-vulnerable thing you'd normally withhold. *(title confirmed.)*
- **Lowering your inner filter (Day 11):** deliberately say more of what comes to
  mind instead of master-planning the "perfect" line — the antidote to
  conversational freezing. *(confirmed as a CU ground rule.)*
- **Speaking through a smile + setting up jokes (Day 12):** break the ice with
  light comedy; talk *as if* you're already smiling/amused. *(confirmed.)*
- **Creating / sharing your values (Days 13–14):** identify what you actually care
  about and bring those up to connect "one level deeper," and share them rather
  than only extracting others'. *(confirmed.)*
- **Speaking with a stranger (Day 15):** a real-world field assignment — start a
  conversation with someone you don't know, using a go-to opener. *(confirmed —
  the canonical CU social challenge.)*
- **Storytelling pipeline (Days 16–20):** overarching question/purpose → hook →
  present tense → rising action (everything serves the point) → expand the
  climax/punchline → wrap-up/moral; build and tell one funny story and one
  purpose-driven story. *(structure confirmed via Power Moves review.)*
- **Open body language (Day 21 – "Keep Yourself Open"):** keep torso/arms open and
  unguarded; notice and remove closed postures. *(confirmed.)*
- **"Catch those free 30 seconds" (Day 22):** use micro-moments (elevator,
  checkout line) to be present and share a genuine smile/connection. *(title
  confirmed.)*
- **Try a new pose (Day 23):** adopt a more expansive, magnetic posture/gesture
  pattern and use brief 1–2s touch to bond. *(confirmed.)*
- **Voice work (Day 24 – "Take It Up A Notch"):** use your *whole* voice, ground
  it (lower/from the chest), expand vocal range vs. monotone. *(confirmed.)*
- **Inspiring vision exercise (Day 26):** write/articulate a vision others would
  want to follow. *(title confirmed.)*
- **Understanding needs / 6 motivations (Day 27):** map the core human drives and
  what someone actually wants. *(confirmed.)*
- **Decide and compliment (Day 28):** counter over-analysis by deciding quickly +
  giving a genuine compliment; antidote to imposter feelings. *(title confirmed.)*
- **Creating win-win situations (Day 29):** frame asks so both sides win.
  *(confirmed.)*

### 4d. The "5 Styles of Charisma" (Day 5 framework, CONFIRMED concept)

CU has students **diagnose their natural charisma style** and then build a plan
that *supports that style* (rather than forcing a one-size extrovert mold). The
styles are built on Olivia Fox Cabane's four types — **Focus, Visionary, Kindness,
Authority** — which CU presents as its set of styles (Kindness/Focus being the
introvert-friendly ones). *(The four underlying types are confirmed; CU's exact
fifth label/naming for "5 styles" is not separately public.)*
