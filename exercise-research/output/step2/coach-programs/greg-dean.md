## 1. Who the coach is

**Greg Dean** is a Los Angeles–based stand-up comedy teacher, often billed as "The
Professor of Stand-Up Comedy," with 40–45+ years teaching joke writing and
performance. He runs **Greg Dean's Stand-Up Comedy Classes** (Santa Monica + Zoom)
and an online **Greg Dean Comedy Academy** (Teachable). He is the author of the
widely cited textbook **_Step by Step to Stand-Up Comedy_** (foreword by Steve
Allen; revised edition 2017) and a five-volume **Workbook Series**. Notable
students include Anthony Jeselnik, Whoopi Goldberg, Sherri Shepherd and Christina
Pazsitzky (Christina P).

**Why he matters for this project:** Dean has the most *codified, vocabulary-driven*
joke-mechanics system of any comedy teacher. Where most teachers say "set up an
expectation, then surprise them," Dean dissects the surprise into five named,
diagrammable parts and gives a literal step-by-step factory (the **Joke
Prospector**) for manufacturing setups and punchlines on demand. Almost every
concept in his system maps cleanly to a runnable solo writing or rehearsal drill —
which is exactly what makes him a goldmine for reconstructing exercises.

**Audience:** absolute beginners through working comics; also "humor for business"
(public speakers, professionals who want to be funnier). The free-ebook funnel
("Top Ten Stand-up Comedy Secrets") and the cheap intro courses target people who
have *never written a joke*.

## 3. The methodology (theory of improvement)

Dean's system rests on a few load-bearing claims:

1. **A joke is a machine, not magic.** Every joke = a **Setup** (creates an
   expectation / **1st Story**) + a **Punch** (delivers a surprise / **2nd Story**).
   The hinge between them is a **Connector** — "1 thing with (at least) 2
   interpretations." The setup steers the audience to the obvious meaning (the
   **Target Assumption**); the punch reveals a hidden, compatible meaning (the
   **Reinterpretation**), proving the assumption wrong. These are the **5 Joke
   Mechanisms**: 1st Story → Target Assumption → Connector → Reinterpretation →
   2nd Story. Because the parts are named, jokes can be *diagnosed* (why did this
   die?) and *built* on purpose.

2. **Write setups and punches separately, in that order** — the **Joke Prospector
   Writing System**. Half 1, the **Joke Map**, manufactures *setups* from a topic.
   Half 2, the **Joke Mine**, manufactures *punchlines* for those setups using the
   5 mechanisms. This converts "be funny" (paralysing) into a checklist.

3. **Premise = a negative opinion about a subject.** Comedy comes from what you
   think is *wrong*. You build a **punch-premise** (negative opinion) and its
   mirror **setup-premise** (the positive/innocent framing the audience starts
   with) so the joke has somewhere to fall from.

4. **Perform jokes as scenes, not recited lines.** A joke is "remembered" by
   re-living it through three **Points of View / perceptual positions** —
   **Narrator POV** (talk to the audience), **Self POV** (you, inside the scene),
   **Character POV** (someone/something else inside the scene). This is the basis
   of the **Act Out**. Rehearsing the *experience* (not the words) is why you
   remember material "naturally."

5. **Separate the Creator from the Critic.** His signature rehearsal idea
   (**Critic Split**): if you rehearse while self-criticising, you are *practising
   self-doubt*. So physically split the roles — rehearse the material in one spot
   (Creator), and step to a separate "Critic Spot" only when it's time to judge —
   so the creative pass stays uncensored. He frames the inner judgmental voice as,
   paradoxically, your authentic **comedic voice**, to be channelled (e.g. via
   **ranting**) rather than suppressed.

6. **Structured iteration over inspiration.** Improve via explicit operations:
   write **tags** (extra punches off the same setup), **shorten setups**,
   **sharpen punches**, order a set with **BCAs** (open strong-B, weakest-C in the
   middle, strongest-A last), **hammock/sandwich** weak bits between strong ones,
   and **callback** earlier punches.

In short: **rules + reps + role-separation.** Make the invisible parts of a joke
nameable, drill each part as its own exercise, and protect the creative pass from
the critic.

## 4. Reconstructed curriculum (the codified system, mapped to drills)

### A. Joke writing — the Joke Prospector

**Half 1 — Joke Map (writing setups):**
1. **List Topics** — list subjects you find *wrong* and want to talk about.
2. **Association List** — list concrete, specific details related to a chosen topic (particulars, not categories).
3. **Punch-Premise** — pick a detail and attach a negative opinion (subject + negative adjective; keep it broad, e.g. "Postal workers are incompetent").
4. **Setup-Premise** — write the *opposite* (positive/innocent) view of the same subject.
5. **Write a Series of Setups** — write several plain statements expressing the setup-premise (no jokes yet).

**Half 2 — Joke Mine (writing punchlines):**
6. **Find the Connector** in a setup (the ambiguous word/idea with 2+ meanings).
7. **Name the Target Assumption** (the obvious meaning the setup pushes).
8. **Brainstorm Reinterpretations** — list *many* alternative meanings of the connector other than the target assumption.
9. **Pick & write the Punch** — choose one reinterpretation and phrase it so the **Reveal** lands at/near the end.

### B. Seven "instant" joke formulas (the 7 Instant Joke Writing Techniques course)
Idiom-twists, acronym-twists, comic names, hashtag punches, comic definitions, fake
URLs, comic lists. Each is a fill-in-the-formula generator producing a one-liner in
minutes.

### C. Improving jokes & routines (Workbook 2)
**Tag techniques** (more punches, no new setup) — Technique 1: tag off a new Target
Assumption; Technique 2: tag off a new Connector; Technique 3: tag off a new
Reinterpretation. Plus **shorten the setup**, **sharpen the punch**, **Rule of
Three**, **Routine Builder** (group jokes by premise into routines), **BCAs**
ordering, **hammock/sandwich**, **callbacks/running gags**.

### D. Funny storytelling / performance (Workbook 3, book ch. 6–8)
**Act Outs** (turn a joke into a scene), the **3 POVs** with practice rounds, the
**Rehearsal Process** (re-live the experience, not the words), the **Critic Split**
/ Creator-vs-Critic separation and **Critic Spot**, **ranting**, **crowd work &
riffing**, mic technique.

### E. Being a funny performer (Workbook 4)
Peak performance (record every show, show list, intro, warm-up, strong opening
line), fearless performing (stage fright, going blank, bombing, the **Hurt Line**),
**handling hecklers** (pre-written comebacks / heckler lines).

### F. Career (Workbook 5 / "10 Steps to Be a Working Comedian") — getting stage time, MC-ing. (Business-track; few discrete *practice drills*, so lightly covered in the JSONL.)

## 5. Evidence quality assessment

**Overall: HIGH for the writing/joke-mechanics system; MEDIUM-HIGH for performance drills.**

- The **glossary** (stand-upcomedy.com/glossary) is an unusually complete, public,
  first-party definition of every term and is the single best source. The **Joke
  Writing Made Simple** page first-party-documents the Joke Map steps verbatim.
  The **5-joke-mechanisms**, **act-out**, **crowd-work**, **rule-of-three**,
  **reinterpretation** and **joke-structure** glossary pages each confirm a drill
  directly. These are marked `confirmed`.
- The **course curricula** (Introduction to Stand-Up = 10 videos; 7 Instant Joke
  Writing Techniques = the seven named formulas; Joke Writing Made Simple = 12
  videos) are confirmed from first-party Teachable/academy pages.
- The **Workbook tables of contents** (esp. Workbook 2 "Technique 3: Tag Based on a
  New Target Assumption," Workbook 3 "Critic Split Rehearsal Sheets / Creator and
  Critic / Critic Spot / POV practice rounds," Workbook 4 performance/heckler
  sections) confirm the *existence and naming* of each drill, though the precise
  in-book steps are paraphrased/reconstructed — those are marked `confirmed` where
  a glossary or course page also describes the procedure, and `inferred` where only
  the section title is public.
- A few fine-grained procedural details (exact rant procedure; exact phrasing of
  some tag steps) are reconstructed from the section titles + the public model, and
  are marked `inferred`.

No paywalls were circumvented and no accounts were created; all detail above is from
public landing pages, the open glossary, public course-listing curricula, Amazon
book listings, a public LinkedIn article series, and a third-party book review.

**Curriculum reconstruction confidence: HIGH.**
