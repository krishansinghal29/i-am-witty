## 1. Who the coach is

**Todd Valentine** (stage name **Todd V**; born in Longmont, Colorado; studied at Trinity
University in San Antonio and Union College in NY) is one of the most influential
modern **pickup / dating coaches for men**. He spent ~15 years as a forum-obsessed
"student of game" starting around 2000, then became the flagship instructor at **Real
Social Dynamics (RSD)** — widely regarded as "the best of the RSD pack." His 2015 RSD
program **Day Game by Todd** (in which he held an equity share) was his breakout. After
a 2017 falling-out with RSD founder Tyler (Owen Cook), he left RSD and built his own
brand, **Todd V Dating**, under which he sells **The System**, **Verbal Game Academy**,
and a set of masterclasses.

**Why he matters for this project:** Of all the dating-social coaches, Todd V is the
most **verbal-game / conversation-mechanics oriented** — his whole brand thesis is that
attraction is built through *what you say and how you say it*, structured as a
repeatable conversation arc rather than canned routines. That makes his curriculum
unusually rich in **named, drillable conversational skills**: a 5-step interaction
model, a taxonomy of opener types, a teasing/playful-banter formula, a three-mode
**tonality** system, and an explicit solo rehearsal drill ("The Wall"). Much of this
maps cleanly onto conversation, quick-wit, and voice-tonality drills, even though the
surface context (cold-approach seduction) is narrower than our app's. He is also a
heavy **infield-breakdown** teacher (annotated real footage), and a believer in
**isolating a single skill at a time** — both pedagogically relevant.

**Audience:** heterosexual men, beginner through advanced, who want to meet and date
women via cold approach (daygame and nightgame) and improve conversational/seductive
skill. Note: the framing is explicitly seduction/"game," which is tonally far from our
anxious-introvert persona — the *transferable* material is the conversation structure,
tonality, and teasing mechanics, not the seduction objective.

## 3. The methodology (theory of improvement)

Todd V's system rests on a handful of load-bearing claims:

1. **Attraction is built verbally, in a predictable sequence — not with canned
   routines.** Every interaction follows a **5-phase arc**: **Open → Premise → Evaluate
   → Narrative → Close.** You always know "where you are" in the conversation and what
   the next move is, which removes the freeze of not knowing what to say.

2. **Lead with intent and stay in the "judge frame."** Communicate early that you are a
   man *evaluating her* for a role in your life (the **Premise**), then **Evaluate** —
   you judge her merit. This "judge frame" (you have standards, she qualifies to you) is
   the structural opposite of supplication and the engine of attraction.

3. **Polarity beats agreement (push-pull / negative association).** Constant agreement
   and complimenting is predictable and unrewarding; introducing **disagreement,
   teasing, and challenge** ("negative polarity") creates the reward-prediction-error
   that generates interest. But he warns explicitly against **over-gaming** — once she
   likes you, switch from teasing to a collaborative **"us together" frame** or you
   trigger her self-rejection.

4. **Delivery is most of the message (subcommunication & tonality).** *How* you say it
   dominates *what* you say. He teaches three vocal modes — **seeking-rapport tonality**
   (rises like a question, low value), **neutral tonality** (flat), and
   **breaking-rapport tonality** (falls like a command, high value) — plus vocal range
   (not monotone), pacing/cadence, eye contact, and facial expression. The right
   **frame** ("I have value / I'm the authority") naturally produces the right tonality.

5. **Qualification: make her invest.** Have her earn it — ask whether he can trust her,
   tell her he's "not sure" about her, make her convince him. Investment → attachment.

6. **Calibration over scripts.** Read her responses ("baseline vs. plotline,"
   subcommunication) and adjust; authenticity and self-amusement (entertain yourself
   first) keep it non-needy.

7. **Practice by isolating one skill at a time, then drill it solo before live.**
   Hence "The Wall" (rehearse against a non-responsive target) and the Day Game
   conditioning drills (one-week single-focus challenges, minimum daily approaches).

In short: **open → lead with intent → judge/evaluate → build a shared narrative →
close, all carried by polarity and tonality, drilled one skill at a time.**

## 4. Reconstructed curriculum

### A. The System — the 5-phase interaction model
1. **Open** — only goal is to get the conversation going. Six opener types: **super
   direct (sexual)**, **direct**, **direct-friendly** (push-pull / observation),
   **situational** (tease / environment), **opinion**, **impersonal**. Skill-graded:
   beginners use direct openers (avoid "coming across like friends"), intermediates mix,
   advanced go indirect with nonverbal undertones.
2. **Premise (Intent)** — establish early that you're a man evaluating her for a role in
   your life; set the man-assesses-woman frame.
3. **Evaluate** — judge her merit to justify continued interaction; hold the **judge
   frame**.
4. **Narrative** — build the "story of us two meeting," ideally framing the two of you
   together (the "us together" / collaborative frame once she's invested).
5. **Close** — the **Pulling Dynamics** 4-step: **seed** intentions & gauge interest →
   **frame as her win** → **qualify** (have her prove worthiness) → **lead** with
   assumed compliance and a predetermined plan.

Supporting concepts taught in The System: **push-pull**, **qualification**,
**seeding**, **frame control**, **DHV (demonstrate higher value)**, handling **shit
tests** / **last-minute resistance (LMR)** / **self-rejection**, the **self-amusing
mindset**, and the caution against **over-gaming**.

### B. The Wall — solo rehearsal drill (the flagship at-home exercise)
A 3-level progression of talking to a blank wall to desensitize and groove the frame:
- **Level 1:** talk to the wall for ~1 minute to get used to getting *nothing* back
  (no validation), so silence stops rattling you.
- **Level 2:** talk to the wall while establishing the **premise** — e.g. playfully
  tease it ("I like you, but you're so flat / so bland").
- **Level 3:** talk to the wall from a **high-value frame** — speak from the
  perspective that you're wanted, that "the wall likes you," that you're used to great
  social feedback.

### E. Subcommunication & Tonality (taught across The System, VGA, YouTube, podcast)
- **Three rapport tonalities:** **seeking-rapport** (upward inflection / question-like,
  reads low-value), **neutral** (flat), **breaking-rapport** (downward inflection /
  command-like, reads high-value). Drill: say the same line in all three and feel the
  status shift.
- **Vocal range** — speak up-and-down, never monotone (monotone + blank face = rejection
  when teasing).
- **Frame → tonality** — install the right frame ("I'm the authority / I have value")
  and authoritative tonality follows.
- Plus **cadence/pacing**, **eye contact**, and **facial expression** (smile while
  teasing).

### F. Playful teasing (free article + Attraction modules) — 3 steps
1. **Delivery** — the only difference between a tease and an insult is delivery: smile,
   vary tonality, deliver it like a joke expecting a good reaction.
2. **Subtlety** — *less is more*; a short "Really? That's… kinda strange" beats a long
   witty line; brevity creates uncertainty and invites her investment (avoid
   over-gaming / showing your agenda).
3. **Negative Association** ("by far the most important") — instead of the default
   positive/agreeing response, find the negative/challenging angle ("How can you
   possibly live in NYC, it's so busy you'd never sleep"). Built on **negative polarity**
   / reward-prediction-error.

### G. Day Game (RSD-era) — conditioning & mindset drills
Independent review documents these one-week single-focus challenges and field drills:
- **"10 reasons why you're the best thing that can happen to her"** — self-concept /
  value-frame writing exercise.
- **"5 things you want to see in her"** — pre-define your qualification criteria.
- **"1 week thinking only positive"** — mindset conditioning.
- **"1 week don't qualify yourself"** — frame-control practice (stop justifying/
  seeking approval).
- **"1 week of certainty in your language"** — speak in decisive "yes/no" statements,
  no hedging.
- **"Talk to groups in the street"** — desensitization drill to normalize approaching.
- Field rule: **minimum 3 daily approaches**, decide your goal type first (number /
  short set / instant date); the **FRED** attraction model (Focus, Relevance, Emotion,
  Decision); the chase-builder line **"we'll talk about that later."**
