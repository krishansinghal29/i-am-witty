# Onboarding

Final accepted flow:

1. Ask one emotional trigger question: "Where do you want to feel quicker?"
   Options: Group chats, dates, work, friends, on stage, when someone teases me.
2. Start one tiny personalized practice immediately.
   Options: Playful comeback, 60-second story, Yes-And riff, confidence reset, warm-up riff.
3. Show a variable reward: better version, style label, small insight, or unlock.
   Options: Sharper rewrite, "Quick Wit" style label, tiny speaking insight, first-step badge.
4. Ask login after the win: "Save your Day 1 streak?"
   Options: Continue with Apple, Continue with Google, Not now.
5. Ask reminder after login or skip: "When should we warm you up?"
   Options: Before work, before going out, after dinner, 8 PM, not now.
6. Land on Today's Plan.

Notes:

- Do not require login before the first practice.
- Keep onboarding to one question before action.
- Ask OS notification permission only after the user picks a reminder time.
- Keep the tone private, low-pressure, and focused on progress vs. past self.
