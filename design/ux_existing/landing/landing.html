For the quietly funny ones

Most "be funnier" advice means perform, right now. riffy is the opposite — a safe room to fumble, retry, and actually get the reps in.

How it works

Three calm beats per rep

Every exercise runs the same gentle loop. You only control the start — then you react in the moment, so there's no time to overthink it.

01 · BRIEF
📋

Read the moment

A tiny scene sets up — who's talking, what they just said, and the one move to try. Take your time here; this is the only timed-by-you part.

02 · RESPOND
🎙️

Riff out loud

The prompt is spoken and the mic opens on its own. You answer in real time — react, don't rehearse. That's where the spark actually grows.

03 · REFLECT
✨

Get a gentle read

Warm, specific feedback on what landed — plus one Better Way to try next time. Measured against your past self, never a leaderboard.
